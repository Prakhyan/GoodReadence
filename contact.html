Help us understand the problem.

A useful support message can include:

  • Your phone/computer model and browser.
  • What you clicked and what happened next.
  • The file type and approximate size.
  • Whether the problem happens online, offline or both.

You can describe a document without sending the file itself. Avoid including private reading material or personal information you do not want to share.
