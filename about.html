About GoodReadence

GoodReadence is a personal reading space for PDF and EPUB books. It brings a reading library, page navigation, bookmarks, highlights, search, reading goals, themes, page-turn sounds, and ambience together in one browser app.

Designed for your own library

Books you select are processed by the reader in your browser. The inspected version stores the library and reading information on this device. It does not provide accounts, cloud book storage, or a public book-sharing service.

GoodReadence is owned by Prakhyan Nagarajan. It is an independent reading app; no registered company or business is claimed.
