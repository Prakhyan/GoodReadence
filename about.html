The reading room

Good books deserve a little space.

GoodReadence turns the files on your device into a place you can settle into. An intimate library, a book that feels at home on your screen, and the freedom to read in your own way.

Keep it personal.

Your books, notes and reading progress stay in this browser on your device. No account is needed to begin.

Make it yours.

Choose the paper, the desk, the view and the sound. Build an atmosphere that suits the story in front of you.

Stay with the page.

Turn, highlight, draw, bookmark or listen. The tools are close when you need them and gathered quietly below your book.

A more inviting way to open a file

Some books ask for a quiet evening. Others become a companion on the train, a chapter between classes, or a few pages before the day begins. GoodReadence gives those small moments a home. Bring a novel, a textbook, your own notes or a collection of documents, and make room for the kind of reading you want to do.

The experience begins with your library. Familiar covers, a place to continue, and a shelf that grows with the books you choose. There is no catalogue to work through before you can reach your own files. Add something you want to read and start there.

The feeling of a book, with the freedom of a screen

A page is more than a rectangle of information. GoodReadence adds gentle paper edges, a sense of depth and tactile page turns to make a digital document feel more considered. On a larger screen, a two-page spread gives the book room to breathe. On a phone, a single page keeps the next passage within reach.

Prefer to move through a document without turning pages? Switch to continuous scroll. You can choose the view that feels natural for a long read, a quick reference or a study session. Zoom in when the details matter, and move at the pace that works for you.

Your reading room should feel like yours

Set a warm parchment desk behind your book, settle into a dark studio, or keep the space simple with a matte backdrop. Choose a paper theme, soften the brightness, and switch the site's appearance between light and dark whenever you want. The website follows your saved choice instead of changing its look with your device settings.

Sound can make the room feel different too. Rain against a roof, a rolling thunderstorm, a crackling fireplace, shoreline waves, a café, a train compartment or slow piano can become part of your reading ritual. Pick one atmosphere, choose its volume, and let the words remain the centre of attention. Page-turn sounds have their own volume and mute controls.

Leave your own trail through the story

A meaningful page is worth returning to. Keep a bookmark, save a margin note, highlight a passage or draw directly over a PDF with a mouse, stylus or finger. Freehand colour and brush sizes let you underline a sentence, circle a detail, or sketch a connection in the margin.

Your markings belong to your local reading copy. They stay with the saved book in this browser, leaving the original file on your device unchanged. Erase a freehand mark, undo a drawing change, or return to a saved note when a thought needs another look.

A little preparation can make a better read

Sometimes the book you want is scattered across several files. Sometimes you need one chapter from a large textbook, or a sequence of images arranged into something you can read. The preparation tools bring those jobs into the same reading room.

Combine PDFs in the order you choose, extract a page range or an indexed chapter, and turn JPG or PNG images into a book. Optional compression can create a smaller image-based copy when file size is getting in the way. You can read the result immediately or save a copy, while your source files remain untouched.

GoodReadence also opens EPUBs and readable text from Word and PowerPoint files. For documents where the exact design, pictures and page layout matter, a PDF exported from the original application gives the closest match. The goal is to offer useful ways in while being clear about how each format is handled.

Room for concentration, and room for progress

A useful reading tool should support the moments when you want to study carefully as well as the moments when you simply want to enjoy a story. Search a text-based PDF, jump through its contents, keep your last page, and build a set of notes you can return to.

Reading progress, daily goals and local streaks add a gentle sense of momentum. As enough reading time and page-turn information builds up for a book, an approximate time remaining can help you decide whether there is room for another chapter. These tools are there to accompany the habit, without asking you to turn every page into a target.

When you would rather listen, read-aloud uses an installed device voice for supported text. It reads PDF page text or an EPUB/Office chapter, with a stop control whenever you want to return to silence. Scanned pages need a text layer before they can be read aloud.

Keep your library close

GoodReadence processes the documents you choose on your device. You do not need to create an account, upload a book to a reading service, or organise your library around a cloud subscription. The app stores your selected books and reading information in this browser.

After an online visit has cached the app, saved books can reopen offline. Supported browsers offer installation, so the reading room can have its own place on your home screen or desktop. Sounds you have already cached can travel with that offline experience too.

Local storage is still browser storage: clearing it, changing browser profiles or losing a device can remove the library. Keep your original books somewhere safe. GoodReadence gives you a personal space to read them, rather than promising a backup of your only copy.

Independent, personal, and made to be used

GoodReadence is an independent web app owned by Prakhyan Nagarajan. Its direction is shaped around a simple idea: the tools around a book should make it easier to spend time with that book.

From the first cover on your shelf to the marks you leave on a favourite page, the experience is built around your own reading. Bring something you have been meaning to finish. Prepare a chapter you want to understand. Choose a little rain, or no sound at all. Then find your place and make it yours.
