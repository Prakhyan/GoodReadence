Release notes

A reading room that keeps growing.

From clearer pages to new ways to prepare, mark and enjoy your books. Here is what has arrived in GoodReadence.

19 November 2026

Your library, ready to travel.

  • Download and restore a full ZIP backup of books, progress, notes, highlights, drawings and settings.
  • Send a book and its reading data directly to another device with QR pairing.
  • Export margin notes, bookmarks and highlights as Markdown or plain text.
  • Restore previews, file integrity checks and separate copies for conflicting reading data.
  • Personal-cloud connections are prepared; provider sign-in setup is still pending.
18 November 2026

A bigger library. A better reading room.

  • 500 complete book PDFs across 23 genres, searchable by title, author and genre.
  • Add books straight to your shelf; author details, edition credits and available reader ratings in More Info.
  • Colourful home page with clickable book covers and a clearer public-library button.
  • Improved page quality, paper depth, mobile page turns and Midnight mode.
  • Freehand highlights, notes, reading goals and richer reading statistics.
  • Restored paper rustle, refreshed rain and thunder, and improved read-aloud pauses.
  • Better file merging, desk choices and consistent library controls.
  • Aligned library buttons, a simpler book guide and the app version kept in FAQ.
  • Automatic online updates and a reviewed, duplicate-checked collection.
17 November 2026

The foundations of your reading room.

  • Local book storage and progress saved on your device.
  • PDF reading with book-style page turns and reading controls.
  • Initial mobile, browser and offline-readiness improvements.
