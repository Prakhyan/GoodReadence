Release notes

A reading room that keeps growing.

From clearer pages to new ways to prepare, mark and enjoy your books. Here is what has arrived in GoodReadence.

19 November 2026

A cleaner room. More room to read.

  • Refined phone navigation, a shorter About and a new purple-and-white identity.
  • Page previews stay inside their tiles; settings sit neatly at the bottom right.
  • Drawing, box highlights and erasers gathered under Mark up, with clearer bookmark and saved-marks icons.
  • Smoother read-aloud that joins printed lines and lets the device voice follow punctuation.
  • 7- and 30-day reading charts, totals, averages and a daily breakdown, plus a brighter animated streak flame.
  • Full ZIP backups restore books, progress, highlights, notes, drawings and settings.
  • Direct QR device transfer and Markdown or text exports for your notes.
  • Backup tools moved to quieter corners; mobile taps have no blue browser flash.
  • GoodReadence joins Casid Studio, with a quiet hardcover and page stacks that shrink as you read.
  • Restored visible page turns, smoother resizing, centered reader menu and feature panels, and 10 saved cover colours plus a no-cover option.
  • A richer About overview with quick points on the tools, atmosphere and reading experience.
  • Optional weekly backup reminders, EPUB font and spacing controls, and dictionary/Wikipedia lookup.
  • Updated PDF and ZIP engines, stronger script restrictions and automated dependency checks.
  • Personal-cloud connections are prepared; provider sign-in setup is still pending.
18 November 2026

A bigger library. A better reading room.

  • 500 complete book PDFs across 23 genres, searchable by title, author and genre.
  • Add books straight to your shelf; author details, edition credits and available reader ratings in More Info.
  • Colourful home page with clickable book covers and a clearer public-library button.
  • Improved page quality, paper depth, mobile page turns and Midnight mode.
  • Freehand highlights, notes, reading goals and richer reading statistics.
  • Restored paper rustle, refreshed rain and thunder, and improved read-aloud pauses.
  • Better file merging, desk choices and consistent library controls.
  • Aligned library buttons, a simpler book guide and the app version kept in FAQ.
  • Automatic online updates and a reviewed, duplicate-checked collection.
17 November 2026

The foundations of your reading room.

  • Local book storage and progress saved on your device.
  • PDF reading with book-style page turns and reading controls.
  • Initial mobile, browser and offline-readiness improvements.
