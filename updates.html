Release notes

A reading room that keeps growing.

From clearer pages to new ways to prepare, mark and enjoy your books. Here is what has arrived in GoodReadence.

18 November 2026 · Complete PDF library

Full books, ready to read.

  • All ten public-library titles now download as complete PDFs, with every source chapter, story or poem retained.
  • Actual source-edition covers, PDF page counts, short author biographies and expandable edition credits.
  • Original free EPUB editions remain available alongside converted PDFs. Books are downloaded only when you choose them.
18 November 2026 · Reader refinements & classics

Quieter details. A new shelf to explore.

  • Improved the home-page button contrast, moved privacy and ownership lines to the bottom, brightened About, aligned navigation near appearance controls, and added subtle drifting sparkles.
  • Restored optional paper-turn sounds while removing the separate end-click. Added a fresh twelve-minute CC0 rain-and-thunder recording.
  • Fixed update dots so a release is acknowledged immediately and stays acknowledged across pages.
  • Shortened mobile turns, reduced animation canvas copies and queued quick navigation taps.
  • Midnight now applies black pages, including full-page scans; smaller embedded illustrations retain their colours.
  • Read-aloud reads the current page or visible spread, pauses between phrases and stops at its end or when pages change.
  • Replaced the bouncing fire with a layered ember flame that grows with the streak.
  • Added a Public library with ten manually downloadable EPUB classics, original credits, source links and regional rights information. Installing the app does not download these books.
18 November 2026 · Premium reading update

A fuller reading room, with more control.

  • Moved the full About story to the home page, introduced the tagline “Good riddance to bad reads”, and added rounded navigation with unread-release indicators.
  • Merge tools now accept repeated additions of PDFs, Office documents, EPUB and images with ordering and removal controls. Office/EPUB conversion is text-based.
  • Highlighted the reading tools and moved brush size and colour controls to a floating desk palette.
  • Refined stats with one-step and direct-entry daily goals, active daily reading totals, and seven-day bar, line and area charts.
  • Removed page-turn audio, improved mobile cover lettering, added progress-based page stacks and expanded the desk choices, including CC0 photographic wood surfaces.
  • Read-aloud now pauses between phrases and stops when the page or chapter changes. Voice quality still depends on the installed device voice.
18 November 2026 · Latest refinements

A quieter toolbar. More freedom on the page.

  • View, desk, notes, drawing and read-aloud controls now live together in the bottom toolbar. The separate upper strip has been removed.
  • The menu opens above the bottom toolbar, and its settings and other panels open as bottom sheets.
  • Added freehand highlighting with mouse, stylus or touch, adjustable brush sizes, four colours, a stroke eraser, undo and clear-page controls. Drawings are saved with the book.
  • Introduced a simple geometric book-and-bookmark logo across the home page, navigation, favicon and installation icons.
  • Light is the default website appearance. Dark mode follows an explicit saved choice consistently across the reader and information pages.
  • Expanded About, added more product FAQs, refreshed release notes, and brought social links together on Contact, including Prakhyan's LinkedIn profile.
18 November 2026 · Reading and preparation tools

Bring more of your library into the room.

Prepare your next read

  • Merge PDFs in a reorderable file list, extract selected pages, or choose a chapter from a PDF's top-level index.
  • Create a PDF book from JPG or PNG images.
  • Make an optional compressed copy of a PDF. The app explains that this changes pages into images and removes searchable text, live links and editable forms from the copy. Your original stays unchanged.
  • PDFs over 100 MB open the preparation flow. Device-dependent limits help keep preparation manageable.
  • Download a prepared copy or add it straight to your library.
  • Added text import for DOCX and PPTX files, with PowerPoint text following slide order. PDF remains the option for preserving original document layout.

Choose how you read

  • Added automatic, single-page, two-page spread and continuous-scroll views.
  • Continuous scroll keeps no more than four full-page canvases near the reading position.
  • Added local margin notes and saved-note navigation.
  • Added read-aloud for supported text using installed device voices, with stop controls and no automatic fallback to network voices.
  • Added minimal, wood, dark studio, parchment and matte backdrops, plus a remaining-time estimate once enough reading data is available.
  • Improved night rendering to keep ordinary embedded raster images in their original colours while the page is inverted.

Keep it on your device

  • Added installation support, home-screen icons and offline app caching.
  • Saved books and notes can reopen without a connection after the app has been cached.
  • New PDF annotation writes are stored with the book in IndexedDB, with support for reading older saved annotations.
  • Added a durable local copy of reader settings.
17 November 2026 · Sound and navigation

A better atmosphere, with less waiting.

  • Rain uses a clean rain-on-roof recording without added insect layers.
  • Thunderstorm now includes steady rain as well as rolling thunder.
  • Beach uses a later shoreline-wave excerpt with gentle filtering for a quieter feel.
  • Train ambience now uses an Indian train journey, with a later section chosen to move away from the departure.
  • Replaced the piano improvisation with an excerpt from Peace of Mind by Kevin MacLeod.
  • Each installed recording runs for more than ten minutes before repeating. Source and licence details remain available in Sound sources & credits.
  • Removed competing duplicate audio preloading and added on-device caching for selected sounds. Previously cached audio can play offline.
  • The GoodReadence wordmark in the library now takes you back to the home page.
  • Added working social links and icon treatments, now collected on Contact.
17 November 2026 · Page clarity and layout

Sharper books, more room to read.

  • Made the opening screen scrollable, including in shorter desktop windows.
  • Increased PDF rendering resolution with device-aware canvas memory limits.
  • Added lightweight layered paper edges around the book.
  • Improved book fitting and centring when a document opens.
  • Reduced nearby full-page caching to a four-page limit.
  • Launched the live GoodReadence website with automatic updates from the main project branch.
  • Added Prakhyan Nagarajan's ownership details and the 2026 copyright notice.
16 November 2026 · The foundation

The personal library takes shape.

  • Brought the PDF and EPUB reader, local book storage, covers and reading progress into one web app.
  • Included bookmarks, rectangular highlighting, text search for supported PDFs, contents navigation, thumbnails, reading goals and streaks.
  • Included paper themes, brightness controls, page-turn sounds and long licensed ambience recordings.
  • Added About, FAQ, Updates, Contact and a helpful page-not-found screen.
  • Added initial website security headers and safer PDF-loading settings.

Earlier release notes described an unreleased build. GoodReadence is now live; the sections above describe the features currently available.
