About the book

"Cattle and Cattle-breeders" by William McCombie is a historical account written in the late 19th century." The work delves into various aspects of cattle breeding and trading, reflecting the author's extensive experience in the cattle industry and his lineage in this field.

It covers the techniques of feeding cattle, the dynamics of the cattle trade, and provides insights into specific breeds, particularly the Aberdeen and Angus cattle.
