About the book

"Pollyanna" by Eleanor H.

Porter is a novel published in 1913. When eleven-year-old orphan Pollyanna arrives to live with her stern Aunt Polly in a small Vermont town, she brings with her "The Glad Game"—finding something positive in every situation, no matter how difficult.
