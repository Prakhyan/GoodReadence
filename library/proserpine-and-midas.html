About the book

"Proserpine and Midas" by Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley is a verse drama written in 1820 for children, with contributions from her husband Percy Bysshe Shelley. Based on Ovid's tale of Proserpine's abduction by Pluto, this blank verse drama reimagines the ancient myth from a feminist perspective, emphasizing the bond between mother Ceres and daughter Proserpine.

About Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley

1797–1851

Mary Shelley was an English novelist who brought Gothic storytelling into conversation with scientific ambition and human responsibility. Frankenstein grew out of a ghost-story challenge in 1816. She also wrote The Last Man, imagining a future transformed by catastrophe.
