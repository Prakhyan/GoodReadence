About the book

"Foul Play" by Charles Reade and Dion Boucicault is a dramatic novel written during the mid-19th century.

The book centers around the lives of two families, the Wardlaws and the Penfolds, as it delves into themes of trust, betrayal, and the consequences of a single mistake.
