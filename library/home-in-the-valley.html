About the book

"The Home in the Valley" by Emilie Flygare-Carlén is a novel written in the mid-19th century.

The story unfolds around Nanna, a modest and beautiful maiden living in a picturesque valley near Lake Wenner, whose life is marked by poverty and the challenges of social class distinctions.
