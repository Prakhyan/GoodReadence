About the book

"Northern Lands; Or, Young America in Russia and Prussia" by Oliver Optic is a historical account and adventure story written in the late 19th century. The book focuses on the experiences of a group of young American tourists aboard the Academy Squadron as they travel through the Baltic region, exploring the cultural landscape of Russia and Prussia.

About Oliver Optic

1822–1897

William Taylor Adams, pseudonym Oliver Optic, was an academic, author, and a member of the Massachusetts House of Representatives.
