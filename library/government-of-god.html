About the book

"The Government of God" by John Taylor is a theological and philosophical examination of divine governance, likely written in the mid-19th century. The work delves into the contrast between the order of God's government and the chaos of human governance, contemplating the moral and spiritual dimensions of existence and man’s relationship with the divine.
