About the book

"The Life, Studies, and Works of Benjamin West, Esq." by John Galt is a biographical account written in the early 19th century that explores the life and artistic development of Benjamin West, a prominent American painter. The book outlines West's journey from his humble beginnings in Pennsylvania to his significant contributions to the art world, particularly as a key figure in the establishment of American painting.
