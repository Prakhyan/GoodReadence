About the book

"Principles of Politeness, and of Knowing the World" by Chesterfield is a guidebook focused on etiquette and social conduct written in the late 18th century. The work aims to instruct young gentlemen on how to navigate society with grace and charm, detailing the qualities necessary to be well-received in various social circles.

Source subjects: Conduct of life -- Early works to 1800; Etiquette -- Early works to 1800; Courtesy -- Early works to 1800.
