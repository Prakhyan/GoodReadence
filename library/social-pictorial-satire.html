About the book

"Social Pictorial Satire" by George Du Maurier is a work that serves as an insightful exploration of the craft of caricature and social commentary through illustration, reflecting the late 19th-century Victorian sensibility. This book is not merely a collection of illustrations but rather a thoughtful examination of the art form that critiques and humorously depicts the social customs of the time.
