Charles Lamb: A Memoir

"Charles Lamb: A Memoir" by Barry Cornwall is a biographical account written in the late 19th century that delves into the life and character of the English essayist and poet Charles Lamb. The memoir reflects Cornwall's personal recollections and admiration for Lamb, introducing readers to the essence of a man who was not only a crucial figure in the literary world but was also deeply devoted to his sister Mary, whose struggles with mental health defined much of his life.

About the book

"Charles Lamb: A Memoir" by Barry Cornwall is a biographical account written in the late 19th century that delves into the life and character of the English essayist and poet Charles Lamb. The memoir reflects Cornwall's personal recollections and admiration for Lamb, introducing readers to the essence of a man who was not only a crucial figure in the literary world but was also deeply devoted to his sister Mary, whose struggles with mental health defined much of his life.
