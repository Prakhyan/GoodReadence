About the book

"The Different Forms of Flowers on Plants of the Same Species" by Charles Darwin is a botanical study published in 1877. Darwin investigates why certain plants produce flowers with distinctly different structures—some with long stamens and short styles, others reversed.

Source subjects: Plants -- Variation; Fertilization of plants; Heterostylism.
