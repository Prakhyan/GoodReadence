About the book

"The City of Dreadful Night" by James Thomson is a poetic work written during the late 19th century, specifically in the Victorian era.

This long poem delves into themes of despair, existential questioning, and the human condition, using the metaphor of a grim city to explore the darker aspects of life and the struggle for meaning amid suffering.
