About the book

"From Boyhood to Manhood: Life of Benjamin Franklin" by William Makepeace Thayer is a historical account written in the late 19th century. The book chronicles the formative years of Benjamin Franklin, portraying his upbringing, the influence of his parents, and the various challenges that shaped his character and future contributions to American society.
