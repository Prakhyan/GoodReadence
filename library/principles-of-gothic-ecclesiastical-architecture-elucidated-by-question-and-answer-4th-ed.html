About the book

"The Principles of Gothic Ecclesiastical Architecture, Elucidated by Question and Answer" by Matthew Holbeche Bloxam is a detailed treatise on Gothic architecture, likely written in the early 19th century.

This work encompasses an exploration of the various styles of Gothic ecclesiastical architecture, including their origins, characteristics, and the transitions between different styles.
