About the book

"An Humble Address and Earnest Appeal" by Josiah Tucker is a political treatise written in the late 18th century. The work addresses the contentious relationship between Great Britain and its American colonies during a period of escalating tensions, proposing that a total separation from the colonies may be more beneficial than maintaining the current ties.
