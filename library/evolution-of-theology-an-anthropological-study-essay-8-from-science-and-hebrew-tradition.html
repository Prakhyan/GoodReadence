About the book

"The Evolution of Theology: An Anthropological Study" by Thomas Henry Huxley is a scientific publication written in the late 19th century. The work explores theology as a natural product of human cognition, analyzing its historical development and the beliefs surrounding spiritual beings among ancient civilizations, notably focusing on the theological concepts found in Israelite society.
