About the book

"The Parson's Daughter of Oxney Colne" by Anthony Trollope is a short story written during the mid-19th century, a time associated with the Victorian era.

The book primarily explores social class dynamics, love, and personal sacrifice through the lens of a young woman navigating her emotions and societal expectations in a rural English setting.
