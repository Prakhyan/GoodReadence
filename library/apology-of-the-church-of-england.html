About the book

"The Apology of the Church of England" by John Jewel is a historical theological treatise written in the late 16th century. This work aims to articulate and defend the principles of the Reformed Church of England during a time of intense scrutiny and opposition, primarily against accusations of heresy from Catholic critics.

About John Jewel

1522–1571

John Jewel of Devon, England was Bishop of Salisbury from 1559 to 1571.
