About the book

"The Autobiography of Madame Guyon" by Jeanne Marie Bouvier de la Motte Guyon is a reflective spiritual memoir written in the late 17th century. The narrative focuses on the author’s life journey, detailing her deep Christian devotion and the profound spiritual experiences she encountered, including her struggles with societal and religious oppression due to her faith.
