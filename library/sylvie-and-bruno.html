About the book

"Sylvie and Bruno" by Lewis Carroll is a novel published in two volumes in 1889 and 1893.

This unusual work weaves together two parallel narratives: a fairy tale set in the fantastical realm of Fairyland, and a social novel unfolding in Victorian Britain.

About Lewis Carroll

1832–1898

Lewis Carroll was the pen name of Charles Lutwidge Dodgson, an English mathematician, writer and photographer. Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland and Through the Looking-Glass turn logic and language into imaginative play. His nonsense verse includes The Hunting of the Snark and Jabberwocky.
