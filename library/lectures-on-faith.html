About the book

"The Lectures on Faith" by Jr. Joseph Smith is a set of seven theological lectures first published in 1835. Originally delivered to church elders in the School of the Prophets during winter 1834-35, these lectures explore fundamental doctrines of faith, God's nature and attributes, and the Godhead.
