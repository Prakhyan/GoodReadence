About the book

"The Poetaster" by Ben Jonson is a satirical comedy written in 1601.

Set in ancient Rome with the poet Ovid as its principal character, the play became a key salvo in the War of the Theatres—a public feud between Jonson and rival playwrights John Marston and Thomas Dekker.
