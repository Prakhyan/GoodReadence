About the book

"What Is Your Culture to Me?" by Charles Dudley Warner is an insightful address delivered in the early 1870s, likely during the post-Civil War period, a time marked by social and economic transition in America. This lecture candidly explores the themes of culture, education, and the relationship of the educated elite to the broader society.
