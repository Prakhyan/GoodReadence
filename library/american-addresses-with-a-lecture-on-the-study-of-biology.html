About the book

"American Addresses, with a Lecture on the Study of Biology" by Thomas Henry Huxley is a collection of scientific lectures and addresses written in the late 19th century. The work covers topics related to evolution, nature, biology, and the historical understanding of living organisms, reflecting Huxley’s influential perspectives on science and its methodologies.
