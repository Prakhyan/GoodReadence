About the book

"Th' History o' Haworth Railway" by Bill o'th' Hoylus End is a humorous historical account written in the early 20th century. The book details the inception and construction of the railway line connecting Haworth and Keighley, capturing the local community's enthusiasm and challenges throughout the process.
