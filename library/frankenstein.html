About the book

An ambitious scientist and his creation confront the cost of isolation and responsibility.

Atmosphere matters as much as action: unsettling places, uncertainty and emotional intensity shape the experience. Questions of trust, belonging and the limits of human understanding give the story resonance beyond its suspense. Read for the gradually deepening mood without needing to know its surprises in advance.

About Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley

1797–1851

Mary Shelley was an English novelist who brought Gothic storytelling into conversation with scientific ambition and human responsibility. Frankenstein grew out of a ghost-story challenge in 1816. She also wrote The Last Man, imagining a future transformed by catastrophe.
