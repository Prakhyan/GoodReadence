About the book

"Moody's Anecdotes and Illustrations" by Dwight Lyman Moody is a collection of religious anecdotes and illustrations written in the late 19th century. The book compiles various stories and personal reflections used by the author during his evangelistic meetings, aiming to emphasize Christian virtues and faith.
