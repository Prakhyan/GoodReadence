About the book

"A Letter to the Right Honourable the Earl of Derby" by Benjamin Parsons is a passionate polemic written in the mid-19th century. This work takes the form of an open letter addressing the issues surrounding the proposed opening of the Crystal Palace on the Sabbath, arguing against this practice as a violation of religious observance and a moral wrong.

About Benjamin Parsons

1797–1855

Benjamin Parsons (1797–1855) was an English congregational minister. He was known as a political campaigner who involved his congregation.
