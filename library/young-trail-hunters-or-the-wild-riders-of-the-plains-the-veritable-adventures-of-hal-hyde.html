About the book

"The Young Trail Hunters" by Samuel Woodworth Cozzens is a novel written in the late 19th century.

The story chronicles the escapades of two boys, Hal Hyde and Ned Brown, as they learn about life on a stock ranch in Texas and embark on a perilous journey across the Great Plains laden with adventure and discovery.
