About the book

"The Character of the Jew Books" by Richard Carlile is a philosophical and political work published in the early 19th century. This book serves as a critique of the moral and ethical teachings found in biblical texts, particularly those associated with Judaism, delving into the influence of education on human behavior and suggesting that societal ills stem from the misguiding principles inherent in religious texts rather than innate human depravity.

Source subjects: Moral education; Bible -- Study and teaching; Poverty -- Moral and ethical aspects.
