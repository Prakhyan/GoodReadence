The Wyandotte Convention: an address

by John Alexander Martin

"The Wyandotte Convention: an address by John Alexander Martin" is a historical address delivered by John Alexander Martin at the reunion of members and officers of the Wyandotte Constitutional Convention held in Wyandotte, Kansas, in the late 19th century. It reflects on the significant event of the convention, which was critical in framing the Kansas Constitution amid the tumultuous backdrop of the antebellum period, marked by conflicts over slavery and statehood.

About the book

"The Wyandotte Convention: an address by John Alexander Martin" is a historical address delivered by John Alexander Martin at the reunion of members and officers of the Wyandotte Constitutional Convention held in Wyandotte, Kansas, in the late 19th century. It reflects on the significant event of the convention, which was critical in framing the Kansas Constitution amid the tumultuous backdrop of the antebellum period, marked by conflicts over slavery and statehood.
