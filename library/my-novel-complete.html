About the book

"My Novel" by Edward Bulwer-Lytton is a novel likely written during the early to mid-19th century.

The story opens with a discourse among characters in Uncle Roland's tower, where the protagonist, Pisistratus, is urged to write a novel amidst discussions of history, population pressures, and social commentary on English rural life.
