About the book

"Lights and Shadows of New York Life" by James Dabney McCabe is a descriptive work examining the various facets of life in New York City, written in the late 19th century. The book offers a vivid portrayal of the city's splendors and destitution, analyzing its wealth and poverty, societal dynamics, and notable events.

About James Dabney McCabe

1842–1883

James Dabney McCabe Jr. (1842–1883) was an American writer. Along with his birth name, he also selectively used the pseudonym Edward Winslow Martin.
