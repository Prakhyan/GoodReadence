About the book

"Paul the Peddler; Or, The Fortunes of a Young Street Merchant" by Horatio Alger Jr.

is a novel likely written in the late 19th century. The story revolves around a determined young street merchant named Paul Hoffman, who engages in selling prize packages filled with candy and the prospect of prizes.
