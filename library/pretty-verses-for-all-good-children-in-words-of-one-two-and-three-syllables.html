About the book

"Pretty Verses for All Good Children: In Words of One, Two, and Three Syllables" by Lyman Cobb is a children's literature book written in the early 19th century, specifically in 1836. This work is designed as a collection of educational verse aimed at young readers, utilizing simple language structured around one, two, and three syllables.
