About the book

"Trips to the Moon" by Lucian is a satirical work likely written in the 2nd century AD.

This piece primarily comprises comedic narratives that playfully critique the absurdities of human belief and contemporary historiography, employing imaginative elements and fantastic voyages to convey its messages.
