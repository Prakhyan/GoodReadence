About the book

Watson recounts investigations that lead toward a confrontation with Moriarty.

Clues, testimony and conflicting appearances invite you to examine what the characters believe they know. The pleasure lies in following the investigation and noticing details as they acquire new significance. This is a good choice for readers who enjoy atmosphere, deduction and a steadily developing puzzle.

About Arthur Conan Doyle

1859–1930

Arthur Conan Doyle was a Scottish-born physician and author who created Sherlock Holmes and Dr Watson. His detective stories made close observation central to the mystery genre. Beyond Holmes, he wrote historical fiction and adventure stories, including The Lost World.
