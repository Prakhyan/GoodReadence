About the book

"The Dolorous Passion of Our Lord Jesus Christ" by Anna Katharina Emmerich is a book published in 1833, based on the mystic visions of a bedridden German stigmatic. Recorded by poet Clemens Brentano, the work presents an intensely detailed account of Christ's final days, from the Last Supper through crucifixion to resurrection.
