About the book

"History of Friedrich II of Prussia — Appendix" by Thomas Carlyle is a historical account written during the 19th century, specifically in the Victorian era. The book focuses on the life and reign of Friedrich II (Frederick the Great) of Prussia, highlighting his administrative and military achievements, as well as his interactions with his subjects and his efforts in modernizing Prussia through agriculture and infrastructure improvements.

About Thomas Carlyle

1795–1881

Thomas Carlyle was a Scottish essayist, historian and philosopher. Known as the "sage of Chelsea", his writings strongly influenced the intellectual and artistic culture of the Victorian era.
