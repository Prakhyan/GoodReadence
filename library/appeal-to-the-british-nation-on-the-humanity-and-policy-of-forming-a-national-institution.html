About the book

"An Appeal to the British Nation on the Humanity and Policy of Forming a National Institution for the Preservation of Lives and Property from Shipwreck" by Sir William Hillary, Baronet, is a persuasive pamphlet written in the early 19th century. This work advocates for the establishment of a national institution dedicated to the rescue of individuals stranded by shipwrecks, with the intent of addressing the significant loss of life and property that such disasters frequently entail.
