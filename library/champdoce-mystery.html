The Champdoce Mystery

by Emile Gaboriau

"The Champdoce Mystery" by Emile Gaboriau is a novel written in the late 19th century and serves as a sequel to "Caught in the Net." The story revolves around the complex life of Louis Norbert de Champdoce, the son of the eccentric and miserly Duke de Champdoce. As Norbert struggles with the oppressive nature of his father's ambitions and their impact on his own desires, he becomes embroiled in a world of love, societal expectations, and class dynamics.

About Emile Gaboriau

1832–1873

Étienne Émile Gaboriau was a French writer, novelist, journalist, and a pioneer of detective fiction.
