About the book

"Five Weeks in a Balloon" by Jules Verne is an adventure novel published in 1863.

Dr. Samuel Fergusson, his manservant Joe, and professional hunter Richard Kennedy set out to cross unexplored Africa in a hydrogen balloon with a revolutionary altitude-control mechanism.
