About the book

"To Be Read at Dusk" by Charles Dickens is a haunting short story written during the late 19th century, a time characterized by the Victorian literary tradition.

The narrative falls within the gothic genre, as it weaves themes of mystery, the supernatural, and psychological tension, exploring the inexplicable and the nature of belief in ghosts. The story emphasizes the engagement of different characters with their fears and uncertainties, particularly revolving around strange occurrences and their interpretations.

About Charles Dickens

1812–1870

Charles Dickens was an English novelist and journalist whose memorable characters brought Victorian society to life. His stories combine humour, sympathy and criticism of poverty and injustice. His works include Oliver Twist, David Copperfield, Bleak House and Great Expectations.
