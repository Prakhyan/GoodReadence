About the book

Anne Elliot encounters the man she once refused and the possibility of a second chance.

Social expectations, private hopes and the pressure of making a good match give this story its emotional energy. Look for revealing conversations, changing first impressions and the small choices that expose character. It rewards readers who enjoy relationships shaped as much by the surrounding society as by affection.

About Jane Austen

1775–1817

Jane Austen was an English novelist whose sharp observation of family, money and social expectations reshaped the novel of manners. Her six completed novels include Sense and Sensibility, Emma and Persuasion. Beneath the courtship plots, her wit exposes the choices available to women in her society.
