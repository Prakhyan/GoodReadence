About the book

"Barnaby Rudge: A Tale of the Riots of 'Eighty" by Charles Dickens is a historical novel published in 1841.

Set against the backdrop of London's violent Gordon Riots of 1780, the story weaves together forbidden romances, a decades-old murder mystery, and the simple-minded Barnaby Rudge with his pet raven. As anti-Catholic fervor ignites the city, families are torn apart and ordinary people are swept into mob violence.

About Charles Dickens

1812–1870

Charles Dickens was an English novelist and journalist whose memorable characters brought Victorian society to life. His stories combine humour, sympathy and criticism of poverty and injustice. His works include Oliver Twist, David Copperfield, Bleak House and Great Expectations.
