About the book

"The forged coupon, and other stories" by graf Leo Tolstoy is a collection written between 1902-1904 and published in 1910.

The title novella follows a schoolboy's desperate act of forging a bond coupon to repay a debt. This single deception triggers a devastating chain reaction, touching dozens of lives and ultimately leading to murder.
