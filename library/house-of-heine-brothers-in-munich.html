About the book

"The House of Heine Brothers, in Munich" by Anthony Trollope is a short story set in the mid-to-late 19th century, during the Victorian era.

The story revolves around the modest banking house of Heine Brothers in Munich, which is characterized by its good but unremarkable reputation in the banking world.
