About the book

"Capitola the Madcap" by Emma Dorothy Eliza Nevitte Southworth is a serial novel first published in 1859.

The story follows Capitola Black, a tomboyish orphan rescued from New York's streets by wealthy Major Warfield, who makes her heiress to his Virginia plantation.
