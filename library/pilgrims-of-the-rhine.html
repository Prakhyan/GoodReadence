About the book

"The Pilgrims of the Rhine" by Edward Bulwer Lytton is a romantic novel written during the early 19th century.

The book features both enchanted fairy characters, such as Queen Nymphalin, and mortal protagonists like Trevylyan and Gertrude, whose intertwined fates lead them on a journey along the Rhine River.
