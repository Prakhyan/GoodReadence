About the book

"Prose Idylls, New and Old" by Charles Kingsley is a collection of essays written in the late 19th century that reflects on nature, culture, and the simplicity of rural life.

Through his prose, Kingsley explores themes of ecology, the changing relationship between humanity and nature, and the nostalgic yearning for the connection to the seasons that has been lost in the modern world.
