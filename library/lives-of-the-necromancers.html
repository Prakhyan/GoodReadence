About the book

"Lives of the Necromancers" by William Godwin is a historical account published in 1834.

Godwin's final book examines paranormal legends and magical beliefs from western and Middle Eastern history, tracing tales of sorcery, witchcraft, and supernatural powers from ancient civilizations through the Dark Ages.
