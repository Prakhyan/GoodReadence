About the book

"Autobiography of Ma-ka-tai-me-she-kia-kiak, or Black Hawk" by Black Hawk is a historical account written during the early 19th century. This narrative provides a personal and detailed perspective from the viewpoint of the Sauk chief, Black Hawk, covering his life experiences, the cultural traditions of his nation, and his involvement in the conflicts with European settlers, particularly focusing on the Black Hawk War of 1832.
