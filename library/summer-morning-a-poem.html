About the book

"Summer Morning: A poem by Thomas Miller" is a lyrical poem written in the early 19th century, reflecting the poetic styles of the Romantic period. The poem captures the serene beauty of a summer morning, focusing on the sights and sounds of nature as well as themes of nostalgia and identity.
