About the book

"The Andes and the Amazon: Or, Across the Continent of South America" by James Orton is a travel narrative and scientific account written in the late 19th century.

This volume emerges from an expedition to the equatorial Andes and the Amazon River, aimed at exploring and documenting the geography, natural history, and cultures of South America.
