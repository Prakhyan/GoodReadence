About the book

"The Tiger Hunter" by Captain Mayne Reid is a historical novel written in the mid-19th century. The narrative revolves around the tumultuous backdrop of the Mexican War of Independence, capturing the themes of struggle and heroism through the eyes of various characters, including an inquisitive narrator and the legendary revolutionary figure, Morelos.
