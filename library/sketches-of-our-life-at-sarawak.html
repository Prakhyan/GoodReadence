About the book

"Sketches of Our Life at Sarawak" by Harriette McDougall is a historical account written in the late 19th century. The book chronicled the author’s experiences and observations over a span of twenty years in Sarawak, Borneo, focusing on the progress of missionary work and the interactions with the local communities and the challenges faced amidst political strife and cultural conflicts.
