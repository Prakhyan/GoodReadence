About the book

"The Royal Institution: Its Founder and First Professors" by Bence Jones is a historical account written in the late 19th century. The primary focus of the work is an exploration of the Royal Institution's origins, detailing the impact of its founder, Count Rumford, and the key figures associated with the institution, including notable professors like Sir Humphry Davy and Michael Faraday.

Source subjects: Davy, Humphry, Sir, 1778-1829; Rumford, Benjamin, Graf von, 1753-1814; Young, Thomas, 1773-1829.
