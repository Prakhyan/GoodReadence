About the book

"Findelkind" by Louise de la Ramee, also known as Ouida, is a poignant novel likely written during the late 19th century.

Set against the backdrop of the majestic Tyrolean mountains, the narrative centers around a little boy named Findelkind, who is characterized by his dreamy disposition and profound sense of compassion. The book explores themes of innocence, faith, and the pursuit of nobility in a world that often seems indifferent to the struggles of the vulnerable.

About Ouida

1839–1908

Maria Louise Ramé, going by the name Marie Louise de la Ramée and known by the pseudonym Ouida, was an English novelist. Ouida wrote more than 40 novels, as well as short stories, children's books and essays. Moderately successful, she lived a life of luxury, entertaining many of the literary figures of the day.
