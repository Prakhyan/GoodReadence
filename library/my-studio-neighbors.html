About the book

"My Studio Neighbors" by William Hamilton Gibson is a collection of essays written in the late 19th century.

The work offers an intimate glimpse into the joys of nature and the curiosities of life surrounding the author’s rural studio, blending personal reflections with observations of wildlife.
