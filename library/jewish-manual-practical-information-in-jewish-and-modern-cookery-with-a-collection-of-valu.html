About the book

"The Jewish Manual" by Lady Judith Cohen Montefiore is a culinary guide focusing on Jewish and modern cooking, first published in the mid-19th century. This unique work intends to bridge the gap in available cookbooks by providing practical recipes tailored for the Jewish housekeeper, addressing dietary restrictions and traditional ingredients often absent in contemporary cookbooks.

Source subjects: London (England) -- Social life and customs; Jewish cooking; Beauty, Personal.
