About the book

"Haste and Waste; Or, the Young Pilot of Lake Champlain" by Oliver Optic is a novel for young readers written during the late 19th century.

The story revolves around Lawry Wilford, a fourteen-year-old boy who is a skilled pilot on Lake Champlain.

About Oliver Optic

1822–1897

William Taylor Adams, pseudonym Oliver Optic, was an academic, author, and a member of the Massachusetts House of Representatives.
