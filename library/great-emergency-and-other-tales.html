About the book

"A Great Emergency and Other Tales" by Juliana Horatia Ewing is a collection of short stories written in the late 19th century.

The book primarily revolves around the adventures and experiences of young characters, particularly focusing on siblings Rupert, Henrietta, and Charlie, as they navigate life’s challenges and the concept of "emergency" in a playful and imaginative manner.
