About the book

"The Two Captains" by Friedrich Heinrich Karl, Freiherr de La Motte-Fouqué is a romantic novel set during the early 19th century.

The story takes place in a historical context involving the military campaigns of the time, particularly the conflict between Christian and Muslim forces.
