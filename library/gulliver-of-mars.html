About the book

"Gulliver of Mars" by Edwin Lester Arnold is a novel first published in 1905.

This pioneering work combines fantasy and science fiction, following a military man who travels to Mars by magical means. There he explores strange civilizations and falls in love with a Martian princess.

About Edwin Lester Arnold

1857–1935

Edwin Lester Linden Arnold was an English writer. He was the son of journalist and poet Edwin Arnold, and spent his childhood between England and India. His best-known work is Lieut. Gullivar Jones: His Vacation which is one of the earliest examples of the planetary romance sub-genre.
