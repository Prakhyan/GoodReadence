About the book

"Sketches of the Natural History of Ceylon" by Sir James Emerson Tennent is a scientific publication written during the mid-19th century.

This work delves into the rich and diverse fauna of Ceylon (modern-day Sri Lanka), exploring various species of mammals, birds, reptiles, and insects, while providing narratives and anecdotes that illustrate their behaviors and instincts.
