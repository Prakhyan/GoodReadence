About the book

"A Journey into the Interior of the Earth" by Jules Verne is a science fiction novel first published in 1864.

Professor Otto Lidenbrock, an eccentric German scientist, discovers a cryptic message suggesting a path to Earth's center through an Icelandic volcano. With his nephew Axel and guide Hans, he descends into Snæfellsjökull's depths.
