About the book

"The Autobiography of a Quack" by S.

Weir Mitchell is a satirical narrative combining elements of autobiography and fiction, written during the late 19th century. The story follows a rather dubious character named Ezra Sanderaft, who reflects on his life and misadventures while lying in a hospital bed due to a serious illness.
