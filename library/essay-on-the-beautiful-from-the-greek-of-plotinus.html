About the book

"An Essay on the Beautiful, from the Greek of Plotinus" by Plotinus is a philosophical treatise likely written in the late 3rd century AD, focusing on beauty's nature and its connection to the divine and the soul. This book attempts to explore and define beauty, delving deeply into metaphysical concepts aligned with Platonic philosophy.
