About the book

"Ten Years Later" by Alexandre Dumas is a novel published between 1847 and 1850.

The third and final installment of The d'Artagnan Romances follows the legendary musketeers during the 1660s as young King Louis XIV transforms from uncertain monarch to the Sun King.
