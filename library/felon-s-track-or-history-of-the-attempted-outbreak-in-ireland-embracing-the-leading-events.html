About the book

"The Felon's Track; Or, History of the Attempted Outbreak in Ireland" by Michael Doheny is a historical account written in the mid-19th century that reflects on the tumultuous events of the Irish nationalist movement from 1843 to 1848. The book particularly focuses on the Young Ireland movement, highlighting the struggles faced by its leaders, including Doheny himself, as they sought to foster a sense of national identity and independence against British rule.
