About the book

"The Magician's Show Box, and Other Stories" by Lydia Maria Child is a collection of children's stories written during the mid-19th century.

The tales are rich in imagination and fantastical elements, often reflecting a longing for adventure and the wonders of the world through the eyes of young characters.
