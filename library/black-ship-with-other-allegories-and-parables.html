About the book

"The Black Ship: With Other Allegories and Parables" by Elizabeth Rundle Charles is a collection of allegorical tales likely written in the mid-19th century. The prominent narrative centers around two children, Hope and May, who live in a picturesque but isolated island environment and engage in imaginative adventures while facing the shadow of a mysterious threat—the titular Black Ship.
