About the book

"The Green Hand: Adventures of a Naval Lieutenant" by George Cupples is a historical fiction novel written in the late 19th century. The book centers around the experiences of young Ned Collins, a nascent sailor, as he embarks on his maritime journey aboard an Indiaman ship, encountering various challenges and colorful characters.
