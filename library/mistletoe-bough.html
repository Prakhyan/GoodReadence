About the book

"The Mistletoe Bough" by Anthony Trollope is a short story that falls within the genre of fiction, specifically focusing on themes of love and social dynamics.

Written during the Victorian era, the narrative weaves a tale of romantic misunderstandings and familial relationships, set in an English country estate during the Christmas season.
