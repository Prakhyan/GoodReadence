About the book

"A Strange Manuscript Found in a Copper Cylinder" by James De Mille is a novel published in 1888.

A British sailor discovers a prehistoric Antarctic world sustained by volcanic heat, inhabited by ancient creatures and the Kosekin—a death-worshiping society that inverts Victorian values.
