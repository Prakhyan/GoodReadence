About the book

"Nothing Between" by Edward Hoare is a theological discourse presented as a paper read at a conference of the Craven Evangelical Union, likely written in the late 19th century. This work is a reflection on the special doctrines of the Reformation and their significance for the spiritual life of the Church, specifically highlighting the conflict between the teachings of Protestantism and Roman Catholicism.
