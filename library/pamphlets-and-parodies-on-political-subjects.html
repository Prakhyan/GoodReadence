About the book

"Pamphlets and Parodies on Political Subjects" by William Hone is a collection of satirical writings and illustrations produced in the early 19th century.

This work comprises witty and pointed critiques of political events and figures, reflecting Hone's distinctive style of combining humor with political commentary.
