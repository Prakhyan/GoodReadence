About the book

"In the Wilderness" by Charles Dudley Warner is a narrative collection of personal essays likely written in the late 19th century.

The stories capture the adventures and reflections of the author as he explores the natural beauty and wilderness of the Adirondack Mountains, addressing themes of nature, hunting, and human interaction with the environment.
