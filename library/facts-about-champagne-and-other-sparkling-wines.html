About the book

"Facts About Champagne and Other Sparkling Wines" by Henry Vizetelly is a scientific publication written in the late 19th century. The work serves as a detailed exploration of the production and history of Champagne and various sparkling wines from different regions, particularly focusing on France.

Source subjects: Wine and wine making; Champagne (Wine); Sparkling wines.
