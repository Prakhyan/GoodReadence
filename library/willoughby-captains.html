About the book

"The Willoughby Captains" by Talbot Baines Reed is a school story written in the late 19th century. The narrative revolves around the lives of boys at the Willoughby school, detailing their experiences, friendships, and rivalries, particularly focusing on a major boat race between houses that sets the stage for intrigue and competition.
