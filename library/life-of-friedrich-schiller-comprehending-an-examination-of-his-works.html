About the book

"The Life of Friedrich Schiller" by Thomas Carlyle is a historical account written in the early 19th century. This work explores the life, challenges, and achievements of the eminent German poet, playwright, and philosopher Friedrich Schiller, focusing particularly on his youth and the formative experiences that shaped his genius.

About Thomas Carlyle

1795–1881

Thomas Carlyle was a Scottish essayist, historian and philosopher. Known as the "sage of Chelsea", his writings strongly influenced the intellectual and artistic culture of the Victorian era.
