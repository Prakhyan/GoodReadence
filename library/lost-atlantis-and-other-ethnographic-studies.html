About Daniel Wilson

1816–1892

Sir Daniel Wilson FSA (Scot) FRSE LLD was a Scottish-born Canadian archaeologist, ethnologist and author. He served as the third president of the University of Toronto from 1880 to 1892, a role known as president of University College between 1853 and 1901.
