About the book

"Genghis Khan" by Jacob Abbott is a historical account written in the late 19th century. The book provides a detailed exploration of the life and exploits of Genghis Khan, regarded as one of history's most renowned conquerors, and delves into the cultural and societal structures of the Mongol empire.
