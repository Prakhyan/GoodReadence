About the book

"Plain Sermons, Preached at Archbishop Tenison's Chapel, Regent Street" by James Galloway Cowan is a collection of religious discourses written in the mid-19th century. The sermons delve into various aspects of Christian life and morality, addressing topics such as faith, the nature of service to God versus worldliness, and the importance of confession.
