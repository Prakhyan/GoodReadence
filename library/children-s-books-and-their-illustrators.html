About the book

"Children's Books and Their Illustrators" by Gleeson White is a comprehensive examination of the evolution and artistic contributions of children's literature, likely written during the late 19th century. This work delves into the relationship between text and illustration, discussing how artists have significantly enriched children's books, transforming them from mere educational tools to vibrant artistic creations that appeal to young minds.

Source subjects: Children's literature; Illustration of books; Illustrators.

About Gleeson White

1851–1898

Joseph William Gleeson White (1851–1898), often known as Gleeson White, was an English writer on art.
