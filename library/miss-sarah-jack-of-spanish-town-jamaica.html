About the book

"Miss Sarah Jack of Spanish Town, Jamaica" by Anthony Trollope is a novel written in the mid-19th century during the Victorian era.

The story explores the struggles of life in Jamaica, specifically focusing on the challenges faced by an English sugar grower, Maurice Cumming, as he navigates personal and commercial hardships in a decaying colonial environment.
