About the book

"Emilie the Peacemaker" by Mrs. Thomas Geldart is a children's novel written during the mid-19th century. The book centers around Emilie Schomberg, a kind and patient governess, who navigates her role in teaching and nurturing young Edith Parker while developing lessons about kindness and peace in their daily lives.
