About the book

"Frank's Campaign; Or, The Farm and the Camp" by Horatio Alger, Jr.

is a novel written in the mid-19th century. The story centers on young Frank Frost, a spirited boy determined to contribute to his country during the Civil War, while also managing his family's farm in his father's absence.
