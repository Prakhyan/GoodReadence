About the book

"The Way to Geometry" by Petrus Ramus is a scientific publication written in the late 16th century. This book serves as a practical guide for various professions, including astronomers, engineers, geographers, architects, land-measurers, carpenters, seamen, painters, and carvers, focusing on essential geometric principles and applications.
