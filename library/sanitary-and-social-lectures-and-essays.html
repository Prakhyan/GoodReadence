"Sanitary and Social Lectures and Essays" by Charles Kingsley is a collection of lectures and essays written in the late 19th century that examines the intersection of public health, social conditions, and women's roles in society. The initial focus centers on the responsibilities of women in a country parish, arguing that true philanthropy begins at home and must extend compassion and understanding to those within their immediate circles before aiming to assist the broader community.
