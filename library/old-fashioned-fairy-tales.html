About the book

"Old-Fashioned Fairy Tales" by Juliana Horatia Ewing is a collection of original fairy stories written in the late 19th century.

Ewing's tales, while utilizing traditional elements of fairy drama, offer new narratives that delve into timeless themes of virtue, morality, and the human experience.
