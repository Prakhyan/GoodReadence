About the book

"George Walker at Suez" by Anthony Trollope is a short story written during the mid-19th century, specifically in the Victorian era.

This work chronicles the experiences of George Walker, a somewhat unremarkable Englishman, during his week-long visit to the town of Suez, Egypt.
