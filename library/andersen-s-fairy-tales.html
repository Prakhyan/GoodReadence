About the book

"Andersen's Fairy Tales" by Hans Christian Andersen is a collection of enchanting stories written in the early to mid-19th century.

The work showcases Andersen's imagination and moral reflections manifested through his whimsical and often poignant tales, featuring memorable characters and intricate plots.
