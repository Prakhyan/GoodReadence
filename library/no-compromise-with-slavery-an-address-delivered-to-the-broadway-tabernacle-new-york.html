About the book

"No Compromise with Slavery" by William Lloyd Garrison is a powerful anti-slavery address delivered in the early to mid-19th century. Garrison, a prominent abolitionist, uses this speech to articulate the moral imperative of opposing slavery in all its forms, arguing that any compromise with the institution is morally unacceptable.
