About the book

"A Few Days in Athens" by Frances Wright is a philosophical dialogue written in the early 19th century. The narrative unfolds in ancient Athens and revolves around a young character named Theon, who grapples with his beliefs as he is introduced to the philosophies of Epicurus, contrasting them with the Stoic teachings of Zeno.
