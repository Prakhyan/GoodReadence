About the book

"The Works of the Right Honourable John, Earl of Rochester" by Rochester is a collection of satirical poems, songs, translations, and occasional verses written in the early 18th century.

This work showcases Rochester's sharp wit and critique of social norms, particularly surrounding themes such as marriage, love, and human nature.
