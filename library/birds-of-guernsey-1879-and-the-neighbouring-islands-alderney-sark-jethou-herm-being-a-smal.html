About the book

"Birds of Guernsey" by Cecil Smith is a scientific publication written during the late 19th century.

This work explores the ornithology of Guernsey and its neighboring islands, including Sark, Alderney, Jethou, and Herm, with a focus on summarizing the avifauna present in these regions over the author's thirty years of observation.
