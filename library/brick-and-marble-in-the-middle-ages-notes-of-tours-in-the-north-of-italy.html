About the book

"Brick and Marble in the Middle Ages: Notes of Tours in the North of Italy" by George Edmund Street is a travelogue featuring architectural observations written in the mid-19th century.

This work details Street's journeys through northern Italy as he explores and describes various Gothic architectural styles and historical sites.
