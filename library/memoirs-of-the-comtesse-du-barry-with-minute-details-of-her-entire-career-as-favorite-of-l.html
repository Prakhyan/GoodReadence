About the book

"Memoirs of the Comtesse Du Barry" by Baron Etienne-Léon Lamothe-Langon is a historical account likely written during the early 19th century. The narrative centers around Jeanne Vaubernier, known as the Comtesse Du Barry, who rose from modest beginnings to become the favorite mistress of King Louis XV of France, offering a glimpse into the extravagant and often treacherous world of the French court.

About Etienne-Léon Lamothe-Langon

1786–1864

Étienne-Léon de Lamothe-Langon (1786–1864) was a prolific French writer of many novels, apocryphal memoirs, and a controversial historical work.
