About the book

"Itinerary of Provence and the Rhone" by John Hughes is a travel guide written in the early 19th century. The work serves as a companion for travelers visiting the described region, focusing on practical aspects such as noteworthy sights, accommodations, and local customs rather than a simple historical account.

Source subjects: Provence (France) -- Description and travel; Rhône River (Switzerland and France).
