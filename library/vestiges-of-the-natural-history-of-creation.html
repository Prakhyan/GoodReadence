About the book

"Vestiges of the Natural History of Creation" by Robert Chambers is a work of speculative natural history published anonymously in 1844. It presents a sweeping theory of cosmic evolution, tracing the development of everything from the Solar System to human beings through natural laws rather than divine intervention.
