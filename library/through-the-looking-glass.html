"Through the Looking-Glass" by Lewis Carroll is a novel published in 1871.

When Alice climbs through a mirror into a fantastical world, she discovers everything is reversed—including logic itself. In this chess-themed realm, running keeps you stationary, walking away brings you closer, and nursery-rhyme characters come alive.

About Lewis Carroll

1832–1898

Lewis Carroll was the pen name of Charles Lutwidge Dodgson, an English mathematician, writer and photographer. Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland and Through the Looking-Glass turn logic and language into imaginative play. His nonsense verse includes The Hunting of the Snark and Jabberwocky.
