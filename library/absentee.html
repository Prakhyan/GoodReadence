About the book

"The Absentee" by Maria Edgeworth is a novel written in the early 19th century, reflecting the cultural and social norms of its time.

The story explores the lives of Irish landlords residing in England, particularly focusing on the clashing identities and challenges faced by those, like Lady Clonbrony, who seek acceptance in English high society while neglecting their responsibilities in Ireland.
