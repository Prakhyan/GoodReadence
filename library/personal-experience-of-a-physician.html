About the book

"Personal Experience of a Physician" by John Ellis, M.D. is a reflective medical narrative written in the late 19th century. The work recounts Ellis's journey through the medical profession, touching on both his personal medical experiences and his exploration into alternative treatment methods, particularly homoeopathy.
