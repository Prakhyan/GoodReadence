About the book

"The Old Red Sandstone; or, New Walks in an Old Field" by Hugh Miller is a geological treatise written in the mid-19th century. This work explores the Old Red Sandstone formation, detailing the fossils and geological features found in Scotland, particularly focusing on the author's personal experiences with field studies and observations in nature.
