About the book

A castaway builds a solitary life and confronts the limits of self-reliance.

Movement, unfamiliar places and difficult choices carry the reading experience forward. Alongside the excitement, the story asks what loyalty, resourcefulness and courage look like under pressure. Its historical setting also reflects the assumptions of its period, which modern readers may wish to consider critically.

About Daniel Defoe

Daniel Defoe was an English writer, journalist, merchant and spy. He is famous for his novels Robinson Crusoe (1719), Moll Flanders (1722), Colonel Jack (1722), and Roxana: The Fortunate Mistress (1724). He has been seen as one of the earliest proponents of the English novel, and helped to popularise the form in Britain with others such as Aphra Behn and Samuel Richardson.
