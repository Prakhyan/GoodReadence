About the book

"McGuffey's First Eclectic Reader, Revised Edition" by William Holmes McGuffey is a graded textbook first published in 1836. Part of a revolutionary series that shaped American education, this primer taught young students to read using phonics and word repetition within real literature rather than dull memorization lists.
