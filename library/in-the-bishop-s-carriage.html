About the book

"In the Bishop's Carriage" by Miriam Michelson is a novel written during the late 19th century.

The story introduces us to Nancy Olden, a spirited young woman who finds herself embroiled in a series of misadventures in a city teeming with opportunity and mischief. The narrative promises a blend of humor, social commentary, and a touch of romance as Nancy navigates through her escapades.
