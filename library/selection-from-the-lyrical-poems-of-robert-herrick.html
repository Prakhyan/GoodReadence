About the book

"A Selection from the Lyrical Poems of Robert Herrick" by Robert Herrick is a curated anthology of lyrical poetry crafted during the 17th century.

This collection highlights Herrick's diverse themes, most notably his reflections on nature, love, and rural life, evoking a pastoral charm that resonates with readers.
