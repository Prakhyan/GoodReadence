About the book

"All Aboard; or, Life on the Lake" by Oliver Optic is a fictional novel likely written in the mid-19th century. This book serves as a sequel to "The Boat Club," continuing the adventures of the boys at Wood Lake, now focusing on Charles Hardy rather than Tony Weston, the central figure from the previous story.

About Oliver Optic

1822–1897

William Taylor Adams, pseudonym Oliver Optic, was an academic, author, and a member of the Massachusetts House of Representatives.
