About the book

"The Good Gray Poet: A Vindication" by William Douglas O'Connor is a literary essay written in the mid-19th century. The work serves as a defense of the American poet Walt Whitman, particularly in light of his controversial dismissal from a government position due to the perceived indecency of his poetry, specifically his collection "Leaves of Grass".
