About the book

"The Idler in France" by Marguerite Gardiner, the Countess of Blessington, is a travelogue written in the early 19th century that captures the author's observations and reflections on her experiences in France. The book chronicles her journey through various towns, including Nîmes, revealing not just the cultural and historical context of these places, but also her personal sentiments toward their architecture and lifestyle.
