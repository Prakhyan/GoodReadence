About the book

"The Queen's Matrimonial Ladder" by William Hone is a satirical illustrated pamphlet written in the early 19th century.

This work is a political commentary on marriage and the royal affairs of the time, reflecting the societal norms and expectations surrounding marriage, especially within the context of the British monarchy.
