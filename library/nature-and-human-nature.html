About the book

"Nature and Human Nature" by Thomas Chandler Haliburton is a narrative collection written in the mid-19th century. The book centers around the misadventures and reflections of Sam Slick, the Clockmaker, as he navigates the complexities of human nature and society while on a journey that includes a mixture of humor and social commentary.
