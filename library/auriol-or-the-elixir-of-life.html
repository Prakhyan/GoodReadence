About the book

"Auriol; or, The Elixir of Life" by William Harrison Ainsworth is a gothic novel first published in 1844.

After consuming his alchemist grandfather's elixir, Auriol Darcy lives for over two centuries, bound by a sinister contract with a mysterious stranger who demands women he loves as payment.
