About the book

"Recollections of Bytown and Its Old Inhabitants" by William Pittman Lett is a historical account penned in the late 19th century. The work serves to document and celebrate the early history and significant figures of Bytown, now known as Ottawa, through a collection of reminiscences filled with names and anecdotes from the past.
