About the book

"The Principal Speeches and Addresses of His Royal Highness the Prince Consort" is a collection of significant speeches and addresses delivered by Prince Albert, written in the mid-19th century. This compilation aims to provide insight into the thoughts and character of the Prince Consort, emphasising his contributions to various humanitarian and social causes, particularly focusing on the improvement of the working and labouring classes.

About Prince Consort Albert

1819–1861

Prince Albert of Saxe-Coburg and Gotha was the husband of Queen Victoria and consort of the British monarch from their marriage on 10 February 1840 until his death in 1861. Victoria granted him the title Prince Consort in 1857.
