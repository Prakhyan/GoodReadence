About the book

"The Moon Pool" by Abraham Merritt is a fantasy novel published in 1919.

When a mysterious entity called the Dweller rises from Earth's core to capture humans, Dr. Goodwin and pilot Larry O'Keefe descend into an underground realm of ancient races and advanced beings.

About Abraham Merritt

1884–1943

Abraham Grace Merritt – known by his byline, A. Merritt – was an American Sunday magazine editor and a writer of fantastic fiction.
