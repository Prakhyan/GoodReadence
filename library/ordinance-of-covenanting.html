About the book

"The Ordinance of Covenanting" by John Cunningham is a theological treatise written in the mid-19th century. The book delves into the concept of covenanting as a solemn and religious act of entering into an agreement with God, emphasizing its significance in both personal and social contexts.

About John Cunningham

1819–1893

John Cunningham (1819–1893) was a Scottish minister who served as Moderator of the General Assembly of the Church of Scotland in 1886.
