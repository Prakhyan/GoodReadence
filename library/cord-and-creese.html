About the book

"Cord and Creese" by James De Mille is a novel likely written during the late 19th century.

The narrative begins with the arrival of a letter from England to a man named Louis Brandon in Sydney, Australia, triggering a series of events tied to family tragedy, adventure, and intrigue.
