Lectures and Essays

by Thomas Henry Huxley

"Lectures and Essays" by Thomas Henry Huxley is a collection of educational lectures and essays focused on biology and the natural sciences, written in the late 19th century. This work emphasizes Huxley's thoughts on evolutionary theory, the intricacies of organic life, and the scientific method, particularly in relation to Charles Darwin's "On the Origin of Species." Huxley seeks to clarify and critique the existing scientific understanding of his time, engaging with both the praises and criticisms directed at evolutionary ideas.
