About the book

"Our Sabbaths in Danger" by Daniel Moore is a sermon written in the early 19th century that addresses the moral and spiritual implications of opening the Crystal Palace on the Lord's Day. This theological work discusses the sanctity of the Sabbath, its historical origins, and the consequences of its desecration, reflecting a period of intense religious sentiment characteristic of the Victorian era.
