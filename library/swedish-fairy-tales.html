About the book

"Swedish Fairy Tales" by Herman Hofberg is a collection of folkloric tales written in the late 19th century.

This compilation gathers various traditional stories from Sweden, exploring themes of magic, giants, and whimsical adventures that reflect the deep-rooted culture and mythology of the Swedish people.
