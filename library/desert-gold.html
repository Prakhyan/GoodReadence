About the book

"Desert Gold" by Zane Grey is a novel written in the early 20th century.

It explores themes of love, remorse, and survival against the backdrop of the harsh desert landscape, following a cast of characters intertwined by their pasts. The central figures include Cameron, a prospector haunted by memories of a lost love, and Warren, an older prospector who shares a deep-seated connection to Cameron through a tragic past involving a woman named Nell.

About Zane Grey

1872–1939

Zane Grey was an American author. He is known for his popular adventure novels and stories associated with the Western genre in literature and the arts; he idealized the American frontier. Riders of the Purple Sage (1912) was his best-selling book.
