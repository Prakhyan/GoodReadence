About the book

"Six to Sixteen: A Story for Girls" by Juliana Horatia Ewing is a fictional narrative written in the late 19th century.

The story follows the life of a young girl named Margaret Vandaleur—referred to as Margery—as she navigates childhood and adolescence, exploring themes of family, loss, friendship, and personal growth.
