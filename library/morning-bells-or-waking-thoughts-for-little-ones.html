About the book

"Morning Bells; Or, Waking Thoughts for Little Ones" by Frances Ridley Havergal is a collection of Christian devotional writings aimed at children, composed during the late 19th century. This book serves as a guide for young hearts to start their days with thoughts and reflections centered around faith, encouraging them to not only find rest in Jesus but also to actively follow Him in their daily lives.
