About the book

"The Proportions of Truth" by Edward Hoare is a theological treatise written in the late 19th century. This work examines the essential harmony and balance within religious truths, arguing that exaggeration or distortion can lead to heresy, emphasizing the need for proportionate understanding of scriptural principles.

Source subjects: Church of England -- Customs and practices; Ritualism; Church of England -- Doctrines.
