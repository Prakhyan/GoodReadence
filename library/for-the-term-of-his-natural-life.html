About the book

"For the Term of His Natural Life" by Marcus Andrew Hislop Clarke is a novel published in 1874.

Set in early colonial Australia, it follows Rufus Dawes, a young British aristocrat wrongly convicted of murder and transported to a brutal penal colony.
