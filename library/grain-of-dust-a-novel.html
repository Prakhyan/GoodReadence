About the book

"The Grain of Dust: A Novel" by David Graham Phillips is a work of fiction written during the early 20th century.

The novel introduces its readers to the contrasting lives of two characters: Miss Dorothea Hallowell, an unassuming young woman seeking employment, and Frederick Norman, a young and ambitious lawyer in a prestigious law firm. The narrative explores themes related to social class, ambition, and the complexities of human relationships.
