Two Women, 1862; a Poem

by Constance Fenimore Woolson

"Two Women, 1862; a Poem by Constance Fenimore Woolson" is a dramatic poem written during the late 19th century, encapsulating the themes of love, loyalty, and the harrowing realities of war. The poem juxtaposes the lives of two distinct women—a refined lady and a simple country girl—against the backdrop of the American Civil War, exploring how each navigates their emotions and fates influenced by the conflict.
