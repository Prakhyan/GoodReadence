About the book

"The Greylock: A Fairy Tale" by Georg Ebers is a fairy tale written in the late 19th century.

The narrative follows the journey of a knight named Wendelin as he embarks on a quest filled with adventure, love, and transformation after rescuing a beautiful captive named Clementine from an evil spirit.
