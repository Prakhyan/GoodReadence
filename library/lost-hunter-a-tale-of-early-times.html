About the book

"The Lost Hunter: A Tale of Early Times" by John Turvill Adams is a historical novel written in the mid-19th century. The narrative appears to delve into interactions between early American settlers and Native Americans, centering around a recluse known as Holden, who lives alone on an island and engages with various characters including a wounded young man named Pownal and local Native tribes.

About John Turvill Adams

1805–1882

John Turvill Adams was an American novelist. He was a member of the Connecticut legislature and former lawyer. He died in Norwich, March 30, 1882.
