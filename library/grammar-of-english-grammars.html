About the book

"The Grammar of English Grammars" by Goold Brown is a comprehensive guide on the English language and its grammatical rules, likely written in the mid-19th century. The work aims to provide a thorough understanding of grammar, addressing both common usages and misconceptions while presenting a structured approach for teaching and learning the language.
