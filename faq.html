Help & FAQ

Which books can I open?

The current reader supports PDF and EPUB files. Choose a file from your device in the library. EPUB files are limited to 100 MB, with limits on archive entries and expanded text for device safety.

Where are my books stored?

The inspected reader stores selected books and reading information in browser storage on this device. Browser data can be cleared or evicted. Keep separate copies of books you value.

Can GoodReadence restore my library?

This release does not include a verified complete library backup and restore feature. Browser clearing, storage eviction, profile changes, or device loss can make the local library unavailable.

Are the ambience sounds mixed together?

No. The reader starts one selected recording at a time. Natural background sounds that are part of a source recording may still be audible. The Rain option uses a separate rain recording with no added insect or ambience layers.

Can I share or download books from GoodReadence?

No. GoodReadence is a personal reader. You are responsible for having the rights to documents you open.

How do I get help?

Email goodreadence@proton.me.
