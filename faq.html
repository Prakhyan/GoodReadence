Help & FAQ

Using the reader.

File formats, saved books, backups and the parts that need a connection.

FilesSaving and backupsReadingConnection and installation

Files

What can I add?

PDF and EPUB. JPG and PNG images can be combined into a PDF. DOCX and PPTX import text only; layout and illustrations are not retained. CBZ is not supported.

Does a PDF keep its layout?

Yes. Uploaded PDFs retain their own page layout. Zoom changes the view, not the file. The reader keeps a small page cache instead of rendering the whole book at once.

What is different about EPUB?

EPUB text reflows. Change the font, type size, line spacing, margins and reading view. This reader shows text; embedded pictures and original typesetting may not appear. The source EPUB file is retained in your backup.

How large can a file be?

PDF imports are limited to 160 MB on lower-memory devices and 250 MB on other devices. EPUB, DOCX and PPTX imports are limited to 100 MB and safe archive limits. Combining files has a total limit of 160 or 300 MB depending on the device. Some complex files can still exceed a device’s memory; try a smaller copy.

Can I combine files?

Choose Combine files on your shelf. Combine PDFs and JPG/PNG images, extract PDF pages, or create a smaller image-based PDF copy. DOCX, PPTX and EPUB text can also become fixed reading pages; this does not preserve their original layout.

What happens to a damaged book?

An unreadable file does not remove other books. Export its notes or choose the original edition to replace the file. Missing-file metadata is retained in the recovery section of a ZIP backup. The app cannot repair every damaged PDF or EPUB.

Saving and backups

Do I need an account?

No GoodReadence account is required. Personal books and notes stay in this browser unless you export them, link a backup folder or send a book directly to another device. Cloud-provider sign-in is not enabled on this site.

Will clearing my browser remove my books?

Clearing site data can delete books, notes and progress saved here. Clearing only the browser’s ordinary network cache is different and usually leaves the shelf alone. Keep a ZIP backup outside the browser and test restoring it before relying on it.

What does persistent storage do?

Settings shows whether the browser granted persistent storage. The app requests it after your first saved book or mark. It can reduce automatic eviction under storage pressure; it does not prevent manual deletion, device failure or every browser-specific removal policy.

Why does Safari show an installation notice?

Safari may remove script-saved site data after seven days of Safari use without interaction with the site. Home Screen web apps are treated differently. Add GoodReadence to the Home Screen and keep an external backup; installation is not a guarantee against all data loss.

How do I make a backup?

Open the side menu, then Settings, then Download ZIP backup. The ZIP includes books, reading positions, marks, notes and settings. You can also choose Backup & transfer from your shelf. Restore a ZIP there; existing books are kept unless you choose otherwise.

Can backups go to a folder automatically?

On browsers with local-folder access, link a folder in Settings. While the app is open and write permission remains granted, changes queue a ZIP snapshot, reading-data JSON and Markdown notes in a GoodReadence subfolder. Reconnect if access expires. Nothing runs after the app closes. You may choose a folder synced by your own drive app; GoodReadence does not sign into that service.

How do backup reminders work?

A small reminder appears after ten new marks or notes, or a completed book, when no ZIP backup has been exported in the last week. Dismiss it for a week or download a ZIP. The existing optional weekly reminder is also available in Backup & transfer.

Can I move a book to my phone?

Use Device transfer in Backup & transfer. Both devices exchange QR pairing codes and the receiving device approves the book. A direct WebRTC connection carries the book and its marks. The same Wi-Fi is most reliable; some networks cannot connect directly. Transfer supports up to 128 MB. ZIP import is the alternative.

Reading

Is the opening chapter saved automatically?

No. The landing-page sample is temporary. Save sample to your shelf keeps it as an EPUB. The complete novel is a separate public-library addition.

How do I choose a library format?

The public library defaults to EPUB for adjustable type. Use Add as to choose PDF instead. A library PDF is a reading copy typeset on your device, not a scan of an original printed edition. Uploaded PDFs keep their own layout.

Can I keep both editions?

Yes. EPUB and PDF additions have separate shelf records. Notes are specific to their edition; chapter and page positions are not interchangeable between formats. Existing PDF books on your shelf are kept.

Can I export my notes?

Backup & transfer includes Markdown and plain-text exports. Margin notes, bookmarks and text highlights are included. PDF highlight text is extracted when selectable text exists; visual marks are described by page. Drawing shapes remain in the ZIP backup.

What does Read aloud use?

It uses voices provided by your browser and device, not a GoodReadence voice account. Quality and offline availability vary. In a paged view it reads the visible text and stops; changing pages stops the voice.

Can I change the paper or hardcover?

PDF settings include reading themes, hardcover colours and a cover-free view. EPUB Type controls change text and paper colours. These settings change the reading view, not your original file.

Connection and installation

What works offline?

After the reader has been prepared online, saved books, marks, notes and the information pages remain available offline. Public-library additions and new dictionary or Wikipedia searches need a connection. Sound is available offline only if you downloaded that sound.

How do I install it in Brave or another browser?

Open the side menu on Home or your shelf and choose Install app. Use the browser’s prompt if offered. Otherwise follow the instructions shown: Brave on desktop offers Install in its Save and Share menu; Android browsers offer Install app or Add to Home screen. On iPhone or iPad, Safari’s Share menu has Add to Home Screen. Safari on Mac offers File → Add to Dock.

Are sound downloads required?

No. Sounds stream when chosen online. Download only the sounds you want for offline listening from Settings. Undownloaded sounds are unavailable offline and the app explains why.

Why is a lookup unavailable offline?

Dictionary and Wikipedia requests go to those services only when you choose a word and a provider. Without a connection, the reader shows a message instead. Your book is not sent with the request.

Are all covers original first-edition covers?

No. The catalogue uses verified historical or public-domain edition artwork where available, and original title-cover designs elsewhere. More Info lists cover and edition credits.
