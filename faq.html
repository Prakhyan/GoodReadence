Getting started

What is GoodReadence?

GoodReadence is a personal reading room for documents you already have. Add books from your device, keep a local shelf, choose how the pages look and sound, and return to where you left off.

Is GoodReadence free? Do I need an account?

The current app is free to use and has no account or sign-in requirement. You can open the reading room and choose a file from your device.

Which files can I open?

Choose a PDF, EPUB, DOCX or PPTX, or select JPG/PNG images to create a PDF book. PDF retains its page layout. EPUB and Office documents open as readable text; original pictures and formatting are not reconstructed in those text views.

How do I add my first book?

Enter your library from the home page, choose Add books, and select a supported file. A single book opens after import; multiple books are added to your shelf.

Where can I find the reading controls?

The controls are grouped in the bottom toolbar. It includes page navigation, zoom, bookmarks, rectangular highlighting, View, Desk, Notes, Draw and Read aloud. The three-line menu opens just above it.

Does the app offer books to download?

Yes. Open Public library to browse 500 complete book PDFs. Search by title, author or genre, then choose Add to my library to save a copy on this device. More Info includes edition credits and available reader ratings.

Your library and privacy

Are my documents uploaded?

Reading and file preparation run on your device. Books are not sent to a GoodReadence storage server. If you choose a direct device transfer or an available personal-cloud connection, the selected data goes to that paired device or your own cloud provider. App files, public books and uncached sound recordings still download from the website.

Where are my books, notes and drawings saved?

They are saved in this browser on this device. Book files and newer PDF annotations, notes and drawings use IndexedDB, the browser’s local database. Small appearance preferences are also kept locally.

Will my library appear on another phone or computer?

Each browser has its own local library by default. Move your full library with a ZIP backup, or use QR device transfer to send one book with its progress and notes. Personal-cloud connections will support optional sync when enabled; they require your own provider account.

Can I lose my saved library?

Yes. Clearing website data, using a different browser profile, private browsing, browser storage eviction or losing a device can remove local data. Keep original books outside the app. Use Backup & transfer to download a full ZIP backup containing the original books and saved reading data. Keep it outside the browser so you can restore after clearing site data.

Do highlights change my original PDF?

No. Reading marks, notes and freehand drawings are separate local reading data. They do not rewrite the original PDF on your device. Freehand marks are not currently included in a downloadable annotated PDF.

Can I remove a book?

Open the book options on your shelf and choose Remove book, or use Remove this book in the reader settings. Removing it deletes the local saved copy and its associated reading information; your separate original file stays where you saved it.

Reading and marking pages

Can I choose single pages or a spread?

Yes. Use View in the bottom toolbar to choose Single, Dual or Scroll. Single opens one page, Dual shows two, and Scroll keeps pages in a continuous column.

How do I zoom and turn pages?

Use the toolbar buttons or the page controls. On touch devices, swipe to turn and use two fingers to zoom in the flip view. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or Page Up/Page Down; + and − change the zoom. In continuous view, scroll naturally through the pages.

How do I draw a highlight with my finger or mouse?

Choose Draw in the bottom toolbar. Select a colour and adjust Size, then drag over the PDF with a mouse, stylus or finger. Choose Done to return to normal page navigation. Drawing mode uses one-finger movement for marks instead of swiping or scrolling.

Can I undo or erase a drawing?

Yes. While Draw is open, Undo reverses recent drawing changes on the current page. Erase removes a freehand stroke you touch, and Clear page removes that page’s freehand strokes. You can undo a clear while the current reading session is still open. The separate rectangular highlighter has its own eraser.

How do margin notes work?

Choose Notes while reading a PDF from your shelf. Write a note for the current page and save it. The notes panel lists saved notes so you can return to those pages.

Does the reader remember my page?

The app stores your reading position with the local book. Open it again from the same browser and device to continue. Bookmarks give you an additional way to return to specific pages.

Why can’t I search or read aloud a scanned PDF?

A scan can contain pictures of words without an actual text layer. Search and read-aloud need text that can be extracted from the PDF. This release does not include OCR; add a text layer in another tool, then import that copy.

Does read-aloud use an online voice?

GoodReadence selects installed device voices marked as local by the browser. It does not automatically use a network voice. Voice availability depends on your browser and device; if no local voice is available, install one or try a browser that exposes it.

How accurate is the time remaining?

It is an estimate based on recorded reading time and page turns for that book. It appears after enough information is available. Dense pages, rereading and time spent on notes can change the estimate.

Why does the app render only a few pages at a time?

Large books can use a lot of memory if every page is drawn at once. GoodReadence keeps a limited page cache, and continuous scroll creates no more than four full-page canvases near your position. This leaves more memory available on phones.

Preparing and converting books

How do I merge PDFs?

Choose Prepare a book, select Merge books & documents, and add at least two supported files. You can choose files repeatedly to add more PDFs, DOCX, PPTX, EPUB or JPG/PNG images. Office and EPUB content is converted to text-based pages; export as PDF first to keep the original layout. Use the up/down controls to set the order, create the book, then read it or save a copy.

How do I extract pages or a chapter?

In Prepare a book, choose Extract PDF pages or chapters and add a PDF. Enter ranges such as 1–5, 8, 12–16 using the ordinary hyphen character, or select a chapter if the PDF has a top-level contents index. The page ranges you enter determine the order of the result.

Does compression lower the quality of my original file?

Your original file is unchanged. Compression creates a separate flattened image PDF, which can soften page details and removes searchable text, live links, editable forms and accessibility tags from that copy. The tool asks you to confirm this before processing.

What happens if compression makes the file bigger?

The tool keeps the original instead of returning a larger compressed copy. Some PDFs are already efficiently compressed.

What happens with a file over 100 MB?

A PDF over 100 MB opens the preparation flow instead of opening straight into the reader. Preparation accepts up to 160 MB of input on lower-memory devices and 300 MB on higher-memory devices. EPUB/Office input remains limited to 100 MB. Very complex files may still need a smaller chapter or a more capable device.

Can I turn photos into a book?

Yes. Select JPG/PNG images through Add books, or use Turn images into a book in Prepare a book. The preparation list lets you reorder the images before creating a PDF.

Will a Word or PowerPoint file look exactly like the original?

Office import reads document or slide text. It does not reconstruct the original pictures, charts, animations, typography or page design. Export the document as a PDF in Word or PowerPoint when those details matter.

Can I open a password-protected or damaged PDF?

The current import flow expects a valid PDF it can open without a password prompt. Use an unlocked copy you are allowed to access, and check that it opens in another reader if import fails.

Are prepared files saved automatically outside the app?

No. Use Save a copy to download a prepared file, or Add to library & read to keep it in this browser. The original source files remain separate.

Sound and appearance

Why does a sound take time to start the first time?

Sounds stream only when selected online. For offline listening, open Sound downloads and download individual favourites. Each button shows its size; nothing downloads automatically. When offline, sounds you have not downloaded are unavailable, with a message explaining how to download them.

Are different ambience options playing at once?

Only one selected ambience option plays at a time. Thunderstorm intentionally combines rain and thunder in its recording. Some source recordings also include natural background details, such as birds or occasional passengers in the Indian train track.

How long are the ambience recordings?

Every installed recording lasts more than ten minutes before its first repeat. The player uses a short end/start crossfade for the same recording. See Sound sources & credits in Settings for the individual sources and licences.

Can I keep paper sounds without the end click?

Yes. The paper rustle is available again, with its own volume and mute controls. The separate click/thump at the end of a turn has been removed. Ambience has a separate volume control.

How do light and dark mode work?

GoodReadence follows your device’s appearance by default. Use the sun or moon button to choose a different look. Your choice follows you across pages. To follow your device again, open Settings and choose Use device setting.

Is website appearance the same as the reading paper theme?

They are separate choices. Website appearance changes the home, library, dialogs and information pages. Reader settings choose the paper theme and book brightness, while Desk chooses the backdrop. Brightness changes in 10% steps from 30% to 160%; it adjusts the book rendering, not your device’s display setting.

Will night mode preserve every picture perfectly?

It restores ordinary embedded raster-image regions after inverting the page. Complex image groups, masks, transparency and overlapping content may need further refinement. Vector artwork follows the inverted page colours. Choose a light paper theme when exact colour appearance is essential.

Offline access and help

How do I back up and restore my whole library?

In your personal library choose Backup & transfer, then Download full backup. The single ZIP includes original book files, progress, bookmarks, highlights, drawings, margin notes and settings. Keep it in your device’s Files/Downloads or another trusted location outside browser storage. To restore, choose Restore a backup, select the ZIP, review its contents and choose Restore now. Matching books can keep separate reading copies or use the backup version. Other books already on your shelf are kept.

Does a backup stop the browser from clearing my books?

The browser can still clear its local data. A downloaded backup lets you restore that data afterward. It must be kept outside the browser and updated regularly. Sound recordings and app caches are not included. Full archives support up to 1 GB, with a 300 MB limit per book; use selected-book backups for a larger collection.

How do I send a book from my laptop to my phone?

Open Backup & transfer → Device transfer on both devices. On the laptop, choose a book and Create sending QR. On the phone, choose Scan a pairing code, scan the laptop’s QR, and show the reply QR to the laptop. Scan or paste that reply on the laptop, then accept the named book on the phone. The file and saved marks move over an encrypted direct connection and are verified before they appear on the receiving shelf.

Why does device transfer use a reply QR code?

Both browsers need each other’s connection details. Two scans exchange those details without a signalling server or cloud book storage. The same Wi-Fi works best. Different-network mode optionally uses Google STUN, but restrictive networks, VPNs or Wi-Fi isolation can still block a direct connection. Transfers support up to 128 MB including the archive and saved marks. Use a ZIP backup when direct transfer cannot connect.

Can I download my notes and highlights?

Yes. Choose Export notes in a shelf book’s menu or in Margin notes. Download Markdown or plain text. The export includes page/chapter labels, margin notes, bookmarks and available highlight text. Scanned pages and freehand drawings do not automatically become words: visual-only marks are described by page, and their complete shapes remain in your ZIP backup. This does not create an annotated PDF.

Are Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive connections available?

The connection code is prepared, but public sign-in is not active until the provider registrations are completed. Backup, notes export and direct transfer work without a cloud account. When activated, sync will use your provider’s app folder, with books uploaded once and smaller reading-data updates afterward. It runs while the app is open and signed in. OneDrive work/school accounts are not supported by this personal app-folder connection.

What happens if two devices change the same book?

Sync keeps concurrent reading copies separately instead of silently discarding one device’s notes. Cloud changes affecting the currently open book wait until you return to the library. Removing a book locally does not delete cloud copies. Manage those copies through your cloud provider.

How do I read an EPUB offline in my browser?

Open GoodReadence while online, add your EPUB and wait for it to appear on your personal shelf. After the app has finished caching, you can reopen the saved book in the same browser or installed app without a connection. Choose Aa to adjust its typography. Keep a ZIP backup outside the browser because clearing browser data can remove the saved book.

Can I use GoodReadence offline?

Yes. The reader and About, Help, Updates and Contact pages are prepared after an online visit. Saved books reopen without a connection. Sound downloads are entirely optional: download individual favourites while online to use them offline. New public-library books, online lookups and external links still need a connection.

How do I install it on my phone or computer?

On the home or Your books screen, open the three-line menu and choose Install app. Supporting browsers show their install prompt; iPhone and iPad use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Installation and offline sound downloads are managed together in Settings. Sounds never download automatically.

Why does my library look empty in private browsing?

Private browsing and different browser profiles have separate storage. Return to the browser/profile where you added the books. Private sessions may also remove their data when closed.

What should I do if a large book feels slow?

Try Single page, reduce the zoom, or extract the chapter you need into a smaller PDF. Close other heavy tabs. Compression is another option if you accept the quality and text-layer trade-offs of a flattened copy.

How can I report a problem or suggest a feature?

Use Contact to email GoodReadence. Describe what you were doing, your device and browser, the file type and approximate size, and what you expected to happen. You do not need to send your private book.

Why can an installed iPhone app have a different shelf from Safari?

Safari and a Home Screen app can keep separate local storage. Open the Home Screen app before adding books. If you already added books in Safari, download a full backup there and restore it inside the installed app. Your book files stay private on your devices.

How are public-library books saved?

Choose Add to my library and keep the app open until Ready to read appears. GoodReadence downloads the complete source edition and prepares a searchable PDF on your device, including its illustrations and credits. You can cancel before saving. New books need an internet connection; saved PDFs reopen offline. Page counts and layout can differ from other editions.

Does installing the app download all the books?

No. Installation saves the reading tools, not the public collection. Only books you choose are downloaded and stored on this device. Covers and descriptions help you browse without downloading entire books.

What if a book’s source is unavailable?

No partial book is added. Try again later, or open More Info to visit the original edition for other formats. Books already saved to your private library are unaffected.

How are books ordered in the public library?

Page 1 features the highest verified reader ratings, with rating counts breaking ties. Choose Shuffle books to explore a different selection from the remaining collection. The highest-rated first page stays fixed, and search covers every book.

Make reading yours

Can I change the hardcover colour?

Yes. Open Settings → Book cover and choose from 10 colours, or choose No book cover. The choice applies across your reading room and is saved on this device. Your imported file and library cover image stay unchanged.

Can I change an EPUB’s font and spacing?

Open an EPUB and choose Aa. Adjust the font, size, line spacing, side margins and line width. Your choices stay with that book on this device. PDFs keep their original layout.

How do weekly backup reminders work?

Open Backup & transfer and turn on the weekly reminder. It appears inside the app when a backup is due, and you can snooze it for a week. It does not upload files or send background notifications. Save the ZIP somewhere outside this browser to protect your library.

Can I look up a word without leaving my book?

Select text in an EPUB and choose Look up, or use Look up in the reader tools and enter a word. Choose the English dictionary or Wikipedia. This online feature sends only that word or phrase to the chosen provider.

Can I export my annotations?

Yes. Export notes from a book’s menu or Backup & transfer. Markdown and text summaries include notes, bookmarks and available highlight text. Use a full ZIP backup to preserve visual drawing shapes too.
