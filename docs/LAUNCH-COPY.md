## Product Hunt / AlternativeTo

**Name:** GoodReadence

**Tagline:** A private reading room for PDFs, EPUBs and classic books.

**Website:** https://goodreadence.vercel.app/

**Description:** GoodReadence turns your PDFs and EPUBs into a personal reading room. Read with book-style page turns, adjustable views, notes, highlights and ambient sound. Explore 750 complete classics or bring your own documents. Books and reading progress stay in your browser by default, with ZIP backups and direct device transfers when you want to move them. Free to use, with no account required to begin.

**Categories to consider:** E-book reader, PDF reader, reading, productivity, education.
