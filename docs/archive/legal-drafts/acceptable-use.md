# Acceptable Use Policy — unpublished draft

**Draft status:** Not for publication until the operator, service scope, user age, jurisdiction, and legal review are confirmed. Reviewed 16 November 2026.

GoodReadence is a local-first reading utility in the reviewed build. This draft describes intended responsible use; it does not claim that the operator is an intermediary or that a particular statutory process applies.

Do not use GoodReadence or its website to:

- knowingly infringe copyright or other rights, or use files without permission where permission is required;
- open, create, or distribute unlawful content;
- attempt to exploit malicious files against another person's device or browser;
- probe, disrupt, overload, or bypass security controls protecting the website or its host;
- gain unauthorized access to systems, accounts, or data;
- interfere with other users' devices or misrepresent your identity or authority.

The reader does not host or share users' selected documents in the reviewed build. If a report concerns a threat to the service, include the affected page or feature, approximate time, and steps to reproduce without attaching sensitive documents. Copyright complaints should use the separate reporting draft. Reports may be reviewed and appropriate action taken after verification; no automatic removal or response time is promised.

Contact [goodreadence@proton.me](mailto:goodreadence@proton.me), subject “Support” or “Legal notice”. This policy is a draft and does not replace applicable law or a legal notice-and-action procedure determined by counsel.
