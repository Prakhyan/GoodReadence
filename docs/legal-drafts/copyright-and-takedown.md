# Copyright Complaint and Takedown Procedure — unpublished draft

**Draft status:** This is a proposed contact workflow, not a statement of statutory compliance or a complete takedown process. Do not publish before counsel confirms which laws, notice requirements, response times, counter-notice steps, and operator details apply. Reviewed 16 November 2026.

GoodReadence is the product name. The legal owner of the product, software, brand, interface, and original assets has not been verified. Third-party trademarks and open-source components belong to their respective owners. PDF and EPUB users retain whatever rights they hold. Importing a document does not transfer its copyright to GoodReadence.

The reviewed app does not provide a public book-upload or sharing feature. A report about a user's local file should explain that selected books are processed in the user's browser and are not hosted by GoodReadence.

## Send a report

Email [goodreadence@proton.me](mailto:goodreadence@proton.me) and put “Copyright” in the subject. Provide:

1. your name and a reliable reply address;
2. the copyrighted work and the right or authority you rely on;
3. the precise GoodReadence page, feature, or material you believe is infringing;
4. the basis for believing the use is unauthorized;
5. a good-faith statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act for one; and
6. any other information needed to verify the report.

Do not include a full copyrighted book, identity document, or other sensitive material unless specifically necessary and transmitted lawfully.

## Review

The operator should assess a sufficiently specific report, seek clarification where needed, and take proportionate action only after review. This draft does not promise automatic blocking, removal, a response deadline, or a counter-notice route. The operator, actual service design, applicable Indian and other jurisdiction-specific procedures, and any required contact details must be confirmed by counsel before publication. This draft does not adopt a US DMCA process.
