# Acceptable use — draft, not for publication

GoodReadence is a personal reader. Users must have the right to access the documents they open. Do not use it to infringe copyright, process unlawful material, distribute documents without authorisation, introduce malicious files, probe or compromise the site, gain unauthorised access, or disrupt infrastructure. Report suspected security or rights issues to [goodreadence@proton.me](mailto:goodreadence@proton.me).

This draft does not create final contractual terms. Identify the operator and obtain legal review before publication.
