# GoodReadence identity

GoodReadence is a product of **Casid Studio**. The public ownership line is **© 2026 Casid Studio · GoodReadence**. The product-initiative credit belongs in Help & FAQ.

The current `brand-mark.svg` is the master for the white G-and-bookmark monogram. Its balanced strokes sit on a deep plum gradient with a subtle edge. The familiar silhouette stays consistent from the website to browser, phone and sharing icons. It uses original vector paths, with no stock image or icon font.

The artwork in `brand-mark.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and `icon-maskable-512.png` is dedicated under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). This is a copyright dedication for those assets, not a trademark registration or a licence over the entire application.

To refresh exports after editing the master, run `node scripts/render-brand-assets.cjs`, then `node scripts/render-social-preview.cjs`. Both use Playwright (set `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` when it is installed outside this project). The first command also embeds the same master into the sharing banner. The Android maskable icon uses a full-bleed background and keeps the mark inside the safe area.

Service logos on Contact identify their respective services and retain their owners’ rights.
