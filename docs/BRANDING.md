# GoodReadence identity

GoodReadence is a product of **Casid Studio**. The public ownership line is **© 2026 Casid Studio · GoodReadence**. The product-initiative credit belongs in Help & FAQ.

The logo is the owner's purple page-stack and sparkle design. `assets/brand-source.jpg` retains the supplied reference. `brand-mark.svg` contains clean scalable paths following that design, with a transparent background and tighter framing so the mark stays clear on phones. No CC0 dedication or independent rights verification is asserted for the supplied design.

Run `node scripts/render-brand-assets.cjs`, then `node scripts/render-social-preview.cjs` to refresh the favicon, install icons and sharing banner. Both use Playwright (set `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` when installed outside this project). Android's maskable and iOS exports adds a light full-bleed background and safe margins; the website and regular icons use transparent artwork.

Service logos on Contact identify their respective services and retain their owners' rights.
