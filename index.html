Made for a proper reading ritual.

  • A whole shelf. One search.Find your next classic by title, author or genre and add the complete book straight to your shelf.
  • More than a file on a screen.Paper depth, an understated hardcover and smooth page turns give every reading session a little ceremony.
  • Your comfort, down to the line.Fine-tune EPUB fonts, line spacing and margins. Choose a spread, a single page or a continuous scroll.
  • Keep the thought. Keep the passage.Highlight, sketch, bookmark and write margin notes. Export your reading discoveries as Markdown or text.
  • A word needn’t break the spell.Look up an unfamiliar word in the dictionary or explore its Wikipedia entry, right inside your reading room.
  • Set the scene in a second.Choose your desk, paper, cover colour and ambience. Make the room as quiet or atmospheric as the story needs.
  • Take the chapter with you.Reopen saved books offline, keep a full ZIP backup or send a book directly to another device with QR pairing.
  • A reading habit that feels like yours.Return to your last page, set a daily goal and watch your reading rhythm grow—without creating an account.
