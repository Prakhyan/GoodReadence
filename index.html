§ 01

About the reader

Files, reading views and backups.

01

The file stays with you.

Open a PDF or EPUB from your device. Books, notes and reading positions are saved in this browser. There is no GoodReadence account and no hosted copy of your personal library.

02

The page depends on the file.

PDFs keep their page layout, with single, dual and scroll views. EPUB text can change size, spacing and margins. JPG and PNG images can be combined into a PDF. DOCX and PPTX text import is experimental; export a PDF first if layout matters.

03

A backup belongs outside the browser.

Download a ZIP with your books and marks. On browsers with folder access, link a local folder for automatic backups while the app is open. QR transfer can move one book directly between devices. Clearing site data can still remove anything saved only here.
