Your reading data

Privacy and reading data.

Books are saved in this browser. ZIP exports, local folder backups and direct device transfers happen only when you choose them.

Updated 28 November 2026

Books on your device

GoodReadence stores imported books, covers, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, drawings and notes in this browser’s local database. Preferences and reading statistics are also stored locally. There is no GoodReadence reader account or application server that receives your private book files by default.

Clearing website data, changing browser profiles, device failure or browser storage cleanup can remove this local library. A full ZIP backup includes book files and saved reading data. Keep that file outside the browser and restore it when needed. ZIP backups are not password-encrypted by GoodReadence, so store them privately.

Public-library search and downloads

Public-library searches send the title, author or genre terms you enter to the GoodReadence catalogue service. Results are returned one page at a time. Queries may appear in the hosting provider’s standard request logs. This service does not receive your private book text, notes, highlights or reading history.

Public-library book files are fetched only when you choose to add a book. Some editions download directly from the source publisher; others pass through a GoodReadence download relay for browser compatibility. The relay does not save book files or receive your private books, notes or reading progress. The hosting provider may record standard request details, including the requested public-book identifier. Direct downloads also expose your connection details to that source provider under its own privacy policy. EPUB validation, optional PDF preparation and saving happen on your device. Original source links and credits are available in each book’s More Info.

Local backup folders

Where supported, linking a folder grants this browser permission to write a GoodReadence subfolder containing a ZIP snapshot, reading-data JSON and Markdown notes. Backups run while the app is open and access remains granted. The app may ask you to reconnect after reopening. Unlinking stops further writes and keeps existing backup files.

These files are not password-encrypted by GoodReadence. Choose a private folder. If your operating system synchronizes that folder through iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox or another provider, that service handles those copies under its own policies. GoodReadence does not sign into a cloud account or upload a hosted copy of your personal library.

Direct transfers and camera access

QR pairing exchanges connection details between the devices you choose. Book contents travel over an encrypted WebRTC data channel directly between those browsers. There is no GoodReadence book-storage relay. Same-Wi-Fi mode does not use an external connection-discovery service. If you choose different-network mode, Google’s STUN service receives connectivity requests, including network-address information, to help the browsers find a direct route. Some networks cannot make a direct connection.

The app requests camera access only when you choose Scan. Frames are decoded locally and are not uploaded. Stop camera or close the transfer dialog to release access. The app does not request microphone or location access.

Hosting and external links

Google’s sign-in library loads only when you choose to connect Google Drive. Dropbox and Microsoft sign-in open only when you choose their Connect buttons.

Managing your data

Remove books from the shelf menu, export notes, or restore a ZIP backup. GoodReadence cannot remotely restore books that have been cleared without an external backup. Manage any operating-system-synced folder copies through your own drive provider.

Optional word lookup and reminders

Dictionary and Wikipedia lookups run only when you choose a source. They send your selected word or phrase directly to Free Dictionary API or English Wikipedia, without book files or sign-in tokens. The providers receive normal connection information such as your IP address; their own privacy policies apply. Results stay in memory for the current visit.

Backup reminders use local settings and counts of new marks or completed books. They appear inside the app while it is open. Smart reminders can be dismissed for a week; an additional weekly reminder is optional. Neither sends notifications, uploads files nor runs after the app closes.
