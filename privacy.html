Your reading data

A library you control.

Books stay in this browser by default. Backups, cloud connections and device transfers happen only when you choose them.

Updated 19 November 2026

Books on your device

GoodReadence stores imported books, covers, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, drawings and notes in this browser’s local database. Preferences and reading statistics are also stored locally. There is no GoodReadence reader account or application server that receives your private book files by default.

Clearing website data, changing browser profiles, device failure or browser storage cleanup can remove this local library. A full ZIP backup includes book files and saved reading data. Keep that file outside the browser and restore it when needed. ZIP backups are not password-encrypted by GoodReadence, so store them privately.

Optional personal cloud connections

When available, connecting Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive authorizes GoodReadence to read and write its application folder in your selected account. Book files and reading data go directly from your browser to that provider. GoodReadence does not store another copy on an application backend. Your provider stores these files under its own account, security and privacy terms and may be able to access their contents.

Cloud sign-in uses the provider’s sign-in service. Access tokens are held in memory, not in your exported backup or a GoodReadence account database. Reconnect when a token expires or after reopening the app. Sync runs while the app is open and online. Disconnecting stops this device’s connection; it does not erase cloud copies. Removing a local book does not delete cloud copies. You can revoke the app’s permissions and manage its stored data in your provider’s settings.

Provider policies: Google, Dropbox, Microsoft. Cloud connections remain unavailable until GoodReadence’s provider registrations are activated.

Direct transfers and camera access

QR pairing exchanges connection details between the devices you choose. Book contents travel over an encrypted WebRTC data channel directly between those browsers. There is no GoodReadence book-storage relay. Same-Wi-Fi mode does not use an external connection-discovery service. If you choose different-network mode, Google’s STUN service receives connectivity requests, including network-address information, to help the browsers find a direct route. Some networks cannot make a direct connection.

The app requests camera access only when you choose Scan. Frames are decoded locally and are not uploaded. Stop camera or close the transfer dialog to release access. The app does not request microphone or location access.

Hosting and external links

The website is hosted on Vercel. Loading the app, public books and sound files sends normal web requests to the host. Hosting and security systems can process request information such as IP address, requested path, time and browser details. See Vercel’s privacy policy. Opening an external source, biography or social link contacts that website. Google’s sign-in library loads only when you choose to connect Google Drive. Dropbox and Microsoft sign-in open only when you choose their Connect buttons.

Managing your data

You can remove local books in the shelf menu, clear local website data through your browser, export notes as Markdown or text, and download a full ZIP backup. Keep copies of important documents outside browser storage. For optional cloud data, use your provider’s account controls. GoodReadence cannot remotely restore a local library that has been cleared without a backup or connected cloud copy.
